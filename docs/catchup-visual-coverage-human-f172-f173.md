# Human Biology F172–F173: focused visual review

This record covers the exact blocks listed below; it is not a complete audit of both units. The original SVGs and their text alternatives and captions are registered in `public/human-biology/f172-f173-evidence-figures.js`. Course scope was checked against the [OCR Cambridge Advanced National in Human Biology specification](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf). Supporting sources are [MedlinePlus on interpreting genetic tests](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/understanding/testing/interpretingresults/), [NHS Genomics Education on consent conversations](https://www.genomicseducation.hee.nhs.uk/genotes/knowledge-hub/the-consent-conversation-for-genomic-testing/), [MedlinePlus on gene therapy methods](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/understanding/therapy/procedures/) and [FDA guidance on genome editing in gene therapy](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/human-gene-therapy-products-incorporating-human-genome-editing). The diagrams are original schematics and do not present clinical measurements or experimental results.
