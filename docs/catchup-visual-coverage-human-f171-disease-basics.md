# Human Biology F171: disease basics recap visual review

This record covers the three reachable F171/a1 short-recap blocks. The index 0 route opens a recap rather than guided teaching and is included. Original diagrams and reused local figures are documented with course and NHS sources in `public/human-biology/f171-disease-basics-recaps.js`. Patient details and numerical risk estimates are not claimed.

| Recap block | Adjacent visual and scientific accuracy | Teaching relevance | Rendered readability and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F171/a1/0: sign versus symptom | `f171-sign-symptom.svg`: an objectively measured temperature and a person's report of tiredness are examples of a sign and symptom. | Compares a measurable finding with a subjective experience. | Actual route checked at 320/1280. Either finding can have several causes; a person can measure an objective sign themselves. |
| F171/a1/1: infectious versus non-infectious causes | `f171-disease-cause-examples.svg`: bacteria can be one infectious cause of pneumonia; arterial plaque is a non-infectious disease example. | Connects the category distinction to two concrete mechanisms. | Actual route and separately grouped clause checked at 320/1280. Neither picture is a diagnosis; pneumonia has multiple causes. |
| F171/a1/1: risk factor and uncertainty | `f171-risk-probability.svg`: two hypothetical groups of ten show 2 and 4 outcomes; both groups include people without the outcome. | Explains that an associated higher chance does not determine an individual's outcome. | Actual route and separately grouped clause checked at 320/1280. Counts are a teaching model, not measured data or proof of causation. |
| F171/a1/2: structure, function and effect | `f171-asthma-airway.svg`: matched outer airway width with a narrower lumen in one example. | Links narrowed airway space to reduced airflow and possible breathlessness. | Actual route checked at 320/1280. The image is schematic; symptoms and their causes vary. |

Independent scientific review checked examples, hypothetical arithmetic and wording. The associated practice route was checked and does not contain the recap figures. Figures do not count as demonstrated understanding.
