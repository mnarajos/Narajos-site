# Human Biology teaching-block visual review

This is a working, **partial** review, not evidence that the Human Biology course is complete. The route inventory is in [catchup-visual-coverage-human.md](catchup-visual-coverage-human.md), the [guided block index](catchup-visual-coverage-human-block-index.md) enumerates the authored explanation and worked-model steps, and the [recap index](catchup-visual-coverage-human-recaps.md) lists short key ideas. Checks below distinguish an inspection of the drawn relationships from a full external specification review. “Model” means the independently navigable worked-example stage; independent assessment does not include these teaching diagrams. Each original SVG has an image text alternative and a caption.

For F170 and F171, a step-0 recap mapping is not visible when that route opens a guided lesson, because those booklets omit the recap disclosure. The integrity check reports these entries separately from reachable recaps. Currently this applies to `f170/1.1.2.counting/0`; its chamber, sampling and dilution figures are checked beside the actual guided explanation. A registry entry alone is not evidence of rendered coverage.


Course scope was compared with [OCR Human Biology specification, version 8](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf): F170 §1.1.1 (specialised blood cells), F170 §1.2.2 (epithelial, muscle, connective and blood tissue; sliding-filament theory is excluded for that statement), F172 §4.1 (gene therapy), F173 §3.4 (chromatography and reference comparison), F175 §4.1 (IVF, ICSI and blocked oviducts), F176 §3.1 (nociception and the sensory/motor reflex route), and F177 §1.3 (receptor activation, agonists and antagonists). These comparisons confirm topics are in scope; they do not establish that each simplified drawing covers the whole specification, and the remaining accuracy checks below stay open.

The direct recaps F170/1.1.2 · 4, 3.2.2 · 2, 1.2.2 · 3, 2.1.3 · 2, 3.1.1 · 3 and 1.1.4 · 3 now group their original words with their respective figures. Each group was checked at 320 and 1280 px for reading order and legibility; the renderer falls back to the complete original text if the source wording changes.
