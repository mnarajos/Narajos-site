# Human Biology teaching-block visual review

This is a working, **partial** review, not evidence that the Human Biology course is complete. The route inventory is in [catchup-visual-coverage-human.md](catchup-visual-coverage-human.md), and the [guided block index](catchup-visual-coverage-human-block-index.md) enumerates the authored explanation and worked-model steps. Checks below distinguish an inspection of the drawn relationships from a full external specification review. “Model” means the independently navigable worked-example stage; independent assessment does not include these teaching diagrams. Each original SVG has an image text alternative and a caption.


Course scope was compared with [OCR Human Biology specification, version 8](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf): F170 §1.1.1 (specialised blood cells), F172 §4.1 (gene therapy), F173 §3.4 (chromatography and reference comparison), F175 §4.1 (IVF, ICSI and blocked oviducts), F176 §3.1 (nociception and the sensory/motor reflex route), and F177 §1.3 (receptor activation, agonists and antagonists). These comparisons confirm topics are in scope; they do not establish that each simplified drawing covers the whole specification, and the remaining accuracy checks below stay open.

## External asset record

- `/assets/micrographs/hb-blood-cells-sem.webp`: [Wikimedia Commons source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_White_Blood_cells.jpg), creator Electron Microscopy Facility, National Cancer Institute at Frederick. Commons marks the image [public domain as a US government work](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-USGov); attribution is not required but is provided. Converted from the 500 × 326 JPEG to WebP with no crop or annotation. The source identifies the colourised SEM subjects as an erythrocyte, platelet and leukocyte. No scale is stated or invented.

- `/assets/micrographs/hb-pancreas-tem.webp`: [Wikimedia Commons source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mammalian_pancreatic_tissue,_nuclear_pores_and_rough_endoplasmic_reticulum.jpg), creator Louisa Howard. Commons marks it [public domain by author release](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-author); attribution is provided. Converted from the 640 × 480 JPEG to WebP without cropping or annotation. The source describes a thin section of mammalian pancreatic tissue and the original 500 nm scale is preserved.
