## Audit checkpoint

The initial register contains 807 authored rows. It indexes every teaching heading, with separate rows for distinct concepts, method demands and key examples. An evidence review classified 50 rows as verified. Candidate and unresolved rows carry no claim of adequate provision.

Existing guided magnification, circulation and exercise, glucose feedback, F171 screening and repeat monitoring, the F175 reproduction model, conditional inheritance and trial comparison are strong starting points. The priority gaps include organelle trafficking, modelling microbial growth graphs from supplied data, choosing physiological measurements, chromatography transfer, distinguishing dietary intake from measured physiological status, antenatal tagging, neural evidence and drug-safety surveillance.

Keep three specification caveats visible during review. The medulla belongs to the brainstem but is not part of the midbrain. The pericardium surrounds the heart rather than serving as the outer layer of its wall. A change in microbiota does not establish a validated treatment for obesity. The existing resources already use the correct medulla and pericardium wording, and an existing question challenges the obesity inference.
