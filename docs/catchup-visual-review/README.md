# Catch-up visual review screenshots

These representative captures record the rendered teaching views reviewed during the visual rollout. KS3, GCSE and A-level images are cropped to the relevant worked card or figure; the Human Biology image shows a stage-specific review guide. Phone views were checked at 320 px (KS3 and Human Biology) or approximately 390 px (GCSE and A-level); desktop views were checked at 1280 px. The captures are examples, not a substitute for the block-level coverage ledgers. The earlier placental screenshots are retained as historical files, but do not represent the current Phase 1 lesson: that figure was withheld pending redesign.
