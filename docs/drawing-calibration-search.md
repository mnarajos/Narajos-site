# Biological Drawing Batch 27 specimen calibration review

## Source of the drawing conventions

AQA 7402 AT e covers scientific drawing from observation with annotations. The January 2026 practical handbook, version 2.3, distinguishes an optical-microscope cell drawing, a map of tissue distribution, and a morphological drawing of structures exposed by dissection. On page 54 it asks for pencil outlines, accurate proportions, an explanatory title and an indication of actual size or magnification. Labelling is task-dependent. Lines should leave the specimen legible. Brief annotations can identify functions or relationships. The activity uses traceable scale bars as its method of indicating actual size. A tissue map of a specified field can finish at the edges of the field. Physical scale still needs evidence. [AQA specification, practical assessment](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/practical-assessment) · [AQA practical handbook, pp. 54–55](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p28bar15/green/2563b719d41442e27a14abbb2ae5360b85ca38c9.pdf#page=55)

## Human buccal epithelial cell

**Decision.** Replace the previous Mulletsrokk overlapping-cell photograph in the drawing judgements. It supported observation of an edge and a nucleus, but its source supplied no physical calibration. The replacement has a single complete cell, a distinguishable nucleus and a printed bar specific to that panel. The old photograph has other uses on the site. [Previous source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cheekcells_stained.jpg)

**New observation.** Figure 5a of *Biotoxic and Cytotoxic Effects of Occupational Pesticides in the Buccal cells of Sudanese Farmers*, Samah Abd-Elrahim Batran, Yousra Waheedaldin Jamal, Hidaya Osman Elzubeir, Alrufaai Musa Ahmed, Hanan Mohammed Sulieman and Yosef Mohamed-Azzam Zakout. The authors identify it as the normal control human buccal epithelial cell, from a Feulgen-stained smear examined by light microscopy. The caption defines the bars as 20 µm. Panels b–f depict other cells with nuclear abnormalities. The scientific source used here is the 11 November 2025 preprint, version 1. The normal control panel and its scale are the evidence used by the activity. [Versioned source](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7919116/v1) · [Publisher PDF, figure 5 on pp. 13–14](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-7919116/v1/81cd3dac-07ee-4698-bde3-2743e791f7df.pdf) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Privet transverse section

**Decision.** Keep the Berkshire Community College midrib as a focused exercise on boundaries and relative proportions. It shows both surfaces, a thin upper epidermal band and the central vascular bundle in a single field. The original reports 100× acquisition, without a micrometre bar or pixel-to-specimen calibration. A student can compare the band thickness and the position of the bundle. A complete drawing decision cannot follow from these observations. ## Arterial wall

The former FishtailBob image shows an artery and vein, with the selected artery occupying a small region. Its lumen and surrounding muscular tissue can support a relative-thickness exercise. It lacks a physical calibration for the selected crop. [Previous source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ArteryVeinCS.jpg)

The 2012 Rauch et al. carotid study was examined as an alternative. Figure 2 includes a calibrated section from a wild-type mouse, but both illustrated vessels had undergone injury, and the new intima is a major feature. Figure 4A–C shows uninjured controls with molecular fluorescence. Although the muscular ring is visible, the outer wall is difficult to distinguish against the dark field. These panels are poor substitutions for the normal wall and lumen task. The selected Batch 27 arterial observation and its calibration are documented separately. [2012 source, figures 2 and 4](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0029904) · [Selected arterial source](drawing-artery-source.md)
