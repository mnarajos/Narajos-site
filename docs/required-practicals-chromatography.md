# RP7 leaf-pigment chromatography

The [activity](https://narajos.com/alevel/#practical-7) shows the TLC apparatus, a worked calculation and a measurable photograph. The existing [paper chromatography catch-up](https://narajos.com/alevel/#catchup-a5-chromat) and its question bank remain available. Students use the left lane for two different Rf estimates, then compare the observed separations in the outer lanes.

## Sources

- The specification asks students to use chromatography to investigate leaf pigments from different plants. The AQA exemplar uses paper chromatography, with the sample origin above the solvent and the solvent front marked. [AQA practical assessment](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/practical-assessment) · [AQA RP7 apparatus set-up guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P7.PDF)

## Media and measurement

Their version 2 article is published under CC BY 4.0 and the figure has no separate restriction. The labels, reference guides and moving line are rendered separately in the shared SVG coordinate space. [Article and source figure](https://doi.org/10.32388/I14RGE.2) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- `public/assets/required-practicals/rp7-tlc.svg` is an original apparatus schematic based on the TLC setup in the method references above.
- The paper identifies the outer lanes as green and red leaf samples and the materials as American basswood and copperleaf, respectively. The left-lane pencil origin and solvent front lie near y = 820 and 60 in the crop. The centre of the highest distinct spot is near y = 260 and the lowest distinct spot near y = 385. These support estimates of 0.74 and 0.57 with a tolerance of 0.02. The slight slope in the reference lines makes the left lane the reference for both calculations. The large right-lane red spot centred near the origin supports the comparison question. The pixel-based ruler is for ratios only. The task assesses the visible migration and comparison, with pigment identity discussed as a separate inference requiring matched reference conditions and further evidence. [Source figure and method](https://www.qeios.com/read/I14RGE.2/pdf)

## Guided paper chromatography simulation

The [guided RP7 simulation](https://narajos.com/alevel/chromatography/) is linked from the RP7 practical and its [catch-up lesson](https://narajos.com/alevel/#catchup-a5-chromat). It retains the sequence of method and mechanism, procedural mistakes, paired comparisons and unfamiliar chromatograms. The photographic TLC activity described above remains separate real-evidence practice, with its image and attribution unchanged.

- [AQA practical handbook](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-PHBK.PDF) and the [AQA RP7 apparatus guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P7.PDF) provide the practical scope and paper setup.
- [Pearson Biology B Core Practical 11, student sheet and Table A](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/A%20Level/biology-b/2015/teaching-and-learning-materials/AS-and-A-level-Biology-B-Core-Practical-11-Chloroplast-Pigments-%28Student%2C-Teacher%2C-Technician-Worksheets%29.pdf) gives paper chromatography references for a 1:9 propanone:petroleum-spirit mixture: carotene 0.95, phaeophytin 0.83, xanthophyll 0.71, chlorophyll a 0.65 and chlorophyll b 0.45. These are examples under specified conditions, not universal constants or an AQA table to memorise. Propanone-only and water values are illustrative. A match supports identification; matched standards and further evidence strengthen it.

The model uses a 100 mm travel region, an illustrative square-root solvent-front trajectory and spot positions based on the model Rf values, with small run-to-run variation. Spots broaden with travel; the wider loading and short-run examples can overlap. Overlapping spots are drawn but their individual centre distances are not presented as measured results. The close-up is a schematic of mobile-phase movement and stationary-phase retention, not molecular timing data. During the l3 question it shows the reference solvent and the carotene/chlorophyll b pair; exploratory solvent and pigment choices remain available in other tasks and are restored when students leave l3. Shade, beet and autumn leaf amounts are authored model specimens; spot appearance alone does not establish absorption or a calibrated pigment amount.

Students can work at their own pace after removing the paper. They mark the front before outlining spots, or explicitly simulate an unmarked front or faded spots. Those accelerated-loss controls are modelling assumptions, not real-time deadlines. A run without a recorded front or resolved spots cannot provide Rf calculation evidence. The linked measurement sequence uses a saved run snapshot for the displayed chromatogram, question and feedback. Because the results table exposes recorded values, its measurement sequence is marked as supported. The standalone prototype saved answers without their experiment data, so it cannot be safely migrated and starts fresh here. State, drafts and evidence are saved in this browser; an interrupted run restores paused, and missing assessment evidence resets its dependent answer and status.
