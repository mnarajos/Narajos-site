# Independent reasoning in AQA A-level Biology

## Audit of the existing routes

The review sampled the Paper 2 passages, Paper 3 evidence and essay lessons, Maths Skills, topic questions and Exam Skills on main at `081136d`. The question was where a Year 13 pupil who knows the biology still has to choose and defend an explanation unaided. The examples below describe what the materials ask pupils to do. Establishing where individual pupils struggle requires their work.

| Assessment demand | Existing strength | Remaining opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apply a familiar mechanism in an unfamiliar setting | Topic passages such as `pass5b` and `pass8b` combine new information with specification knowledge. | Topic titles, named lines and choices can disclose which part of the passage or which mechanism matters. Present a fresh passage in full and ask for an explanation. |
| Evaluate evidence and experimental claims | `stock` targets stock criticisms. `exam-evidence` already asks pupils to evaluate a field claim in writing. | Extend this to a multi-group investigation where pupils choose the comparison and decide how far the design supports the claim. |
| Explain a complete causal mechanism | `exam-causal` models chains and `exam-precision` addresses ambiguous terms. | Remove the supplied endpoint explanation first, then the intervening steps. Ask pupils to connect a specific perturbation to its observed consequence. |
| Identify the exact demand and exclude irrelevant knowledge | `exam-interpret` distinguishes a plausible mechanism from one ruled out by unchanged mRNA. `erel` practises relevance to essay titles. | Give the pupil responsibility for choosing which observations or examples deserve space in their own answer. |
| Select a scientific or mathematical method | Maths Skills and `usedata`, `stats` and `methods` teach calculations, test selection and the reasons for procedural choices. | In a new investigation, leave the choice of groups and numerical comparison open. Judge the comparison by the claim it addresses. |
| Connect different topics | `linkp2` sorts processes by gradient. The written `exam-extended` task already links stomatal closure to root growth. | Change the title and remove suggested processes so pupils must select across topics and explain each contribution. |
| Sustain a precise, coherent essay argument | `ewhw`, `eplan2`, `emini2` and `efull` develop paragraph construction, planning, mini-essays and full essays on paper. | Practise choosing and developing two areas from a bare title, then review each paragraph against the same biological theme. Use the full-essay route for breadth across four or more areas. |

Batch 17 already teaches question interpretation, practical reasoning, precise language, data interpretation and extended answers. The intervention uses the paper routes to give pupils a fresh occasion to exercise those skills. ## Examination evidence

Paper 2 allocates 15 marks to comprehension, with AO2 accounting for approximately 52–56% of the paper. Paper 3 allocates 15 marks to critical analysis of experimental data and 25 to an essay. AO3 includes both judging evidence and refining procedures. ## How the support fades

A pupil can start with the independent question. In guided practice, removing a final step first leaves enough of the worked process visible to locate the decision the pupil must make. Subsequent questions withdraw more decisions. Research supports backwards fading in multistep procedures and shows why success on a similar exercise leaves transfer to a new setting an open question. [Renkl et al., 2002](https://doi.org/10.1080/00220970209599510) · [Atkinson et al., 2003](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.774)

| Route | Model and partial example | Reduced prompt, independent application and reassessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 2 comprehension | A worked coral bleaching passage traces thylakoid damage in the alga to lower sugar production. In the partial example, the opening identifies the coral’s lipid reserves. The pupil explains how these can sustain the coral for a time while its growth slows. | The squirrel passage retains a reading prompt. The independent pea-pod investigation supplies treatment conditions and carbon-13 observations. Pupils decide which comparison explains the seed-starch results. The next passage changes to sensory responses in an aquatic insect. |
| Paper 3 data and practical evaluation | The worked seed study compares uptake, considers variation and identifies a packet confound. An enzyme investigation supplies the interpretation of the observed peak but leaves the design of a follow-up to the pupil. | A second seed investigation removes the worked comparisons and asks pupils to judge a claim about every packet. In the bean-leaf film experiment pupils choose among treatment and humidity comparisons and judge the claim about wilting. A mowing study then requires them to decide what repeated butterfly counts can establish across meadows and years. |
| Synoptic explanation and essays | The model explains how thylakoid and ileal membranes each contribute to cell function. A partial paragraph on starch digestion leaves the biological importance of enzyme action for the pupil. | A haemoglobin opening leaves the mechanism and second protein to the writer. The independent title on removal of substances supplies neither an example nor a paragraph frame. A new title on movement within cells requires a different selection. Each two-area response exercises sustained relevance on the way to the full essay. |

## Review after the exam-year layout changes

The three pathways use the shared Exam Skills renderer from PR #82. The passage, investigation or essay title comes first. A bold question follows the stimulus and any data table, directly above the writing field. Worked examples use the same reading order before showing the current reasoning step. During self-review, pupils can reopen the question, select a specific repair and edit their original response. The completed checklist folds away before the next question. [Mobile and desktop screenshots of all five stages](alevel-paper-review/README.md)

At a 390-pixel viewport, the gap from the bottom of the independent question to the top of its writing field fell from 633 to 34 pixels in the pea-pod task and from 657 to 34 in the leaf-film investigation. Both measurements include the response label. The table and all of its values remain visible before the question.

The scientific review checked the worked examples, all twelve tasks and their criteria. In the leaf-film task, pupils may calculate the percentage reductions or compare the treatment means with two-thirds of the corresponding carrier means. In the meadow task, both sightings averages doubled, while the absolute increases were 12 and 8 per walk. Either comparison can support an evaluation that accounts for the design. The insect model distinguishes a plausible flow of positive charge from what the recordings establish. Essay criteria allow pupils to develop biologically sound examples of their own.

## What would count as pupil evidence?

Before teaching, collect an unseen written response under independent conditions. After practice, use a different, unseen problem with the same reasoning demand. At a later lesson, sample another biological context. Where feasible, alternate which question different pupils receive first and use the same time allowance. Keep each original response before giving feedback. Record hints, retries, model exposure and revisions separately so the teacher can distinguish an unaided first response from an improved draft.

Mark initial responses against each question’s criteria. Look for a defensible comparison or relevant choice of topic, accurate intermediate biology, a justified conclusion and the requested scope. Accept other scientifically valid explanations. Compare changes in those specific features across matched attempts, with a second teacher checking a sample for consistency. For a two-paragraph response, assess the developed examples and their relevance. Reserve the full 25-mark judgement for an independently written complete essay. Completion counts, self-review and browser checks provide no pupil attainment evidence.
