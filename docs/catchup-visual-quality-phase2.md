# Catch-up visuals: Phase 2 quality review

This is a targeted adversarial review of selected teaching routes, not a full scientific audit or a claim of complete coverage. Severity describes the likely effect on learning: **high** means a representation can teach the wrong relationship or make a central mechanism difficult to interpret; **medium** means a label, endpoint or supporting relationship can be misunderstood; **low** means a presentation issue with limited effect on the explanation. Phase 1's block ledgers retain their other open and partial items.

The changed figures use editable original vectors. A photograph or micrograph is valuable when recognising a real specimen is the learning goal; these specific fixes concern relationships, movement and orientation that benefit from simplified explanatory diagrams. This review did not systematically source new micrographs or re-review every existing photograph. Existing questions already ask learners to identify the active-transport direction and antibody target, so the review did not add repetitive checks. Existing question and progress workflows are retained; opening a teaching figure is not evidence of mastery.

The main references for this focused work are the [national curriculum for KS3 science](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study), [AQA Combined Science: Trilogy specification](https://media.aqa.org.uk/resources/science/specifications/AQA-8464-SP-2016.PDF), [AQA A-level Biology specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification), [OCR Human Biology specification](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf), and [OpenStax anatomy of embryonic development](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/28-2-embryonic-development). The exact block ledgers record additional sources and separate checks of scientific accuracy, teaching relevance and readability.

- At 320 px the GCSE `electrode-evidence` observations table can wrap “Electrode” within the word; the evidence remains readable. Review shared table layout separately.
- Existing Phase 1 ledgers contain other partial representations and unreviewed visual-quality questions. This sample and automated checks cannot establish scientific accuracy or accessibility for the entire library. No pupil trial or full manual screen-reader review was undertaken.
