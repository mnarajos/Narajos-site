Counts include alternatives, not distinct objectives. The 14 separate skills families remain available.

A repeat rotates the core question; the three other stages currently remain fixed. Resume preserves the exact queue and draft. Guidance is counted separately from independent success. Written tasks use model/checklist self-assessment, not an automatic examination mark. Errors are explained and retried once at the end; this is an authored sequence, not a validated mastery model. The new bank also feeds mixed/topic practice.

## Remaining coverage work

All 28 F170 and 18 F171 subtopic headings have an entry sequence. Coverage of every teaching statement is **not** claimed. Before claiming completion, expand the audit against both teaching-content and breadth/depth columns, including each named structure, example, mechanism and exclusion.

Priorities beyond these particular sequences include:

- F170 cells: named organelles and their distinguishing structures; cell specialisation; microscopy identification and resolution; all specified membrane processes; mitosis/meiosis stages and outcomes.
- F170 tissues/organs: remaining epithelial and connective tissues, tissue repair, organ histology, named vessels, the specified organ diseases and artificial-organ trade-offs.
- F170 systems: each named system and measurement method, practical action–reason explanations, glucose-tolerance interpretation and ventilation calculations in fresh contexts.
- F170 endocrine/neuro/reproduction: each specified hormone and target, temperature and water feedback, impulse stages within the stated exclusions, brain sections/CSF, reproductive photomicrographs and hormone graphs.
- F170 microbes: bacterial and fungal structures/identification, numerical growth and log graphs, culture validity/safety, viral and bacteriophage features, named beneficial roles and evidence for interventions.
- F171 disease: all specified disease examples, causes, signs and symptoms; macroscopic and microscopic comparisons; distinction between infection, infectiousness and disease.
- F171 therapies: Koch's postulates, casts, surgery/chemotherapy/diet, cellular/molecular transplants, management of the other named disorders and justified prevention choices.
- F171 immunity: barrier mechanisms, named immune cells, gamma-globulin structure, allergy/autoimmunity/immunodeficiency and comparisons of all specified vaccine platforms.
- F171 diagnosis/monitoring: consultation and examination skills, BMI, appropriate further tests, monitoring cohorts, clinical scoring and interpretation of time-series data.
- F171 research/data: manual/electronic sources and their limits, analytics types, qualitative/quantitative methods, feasibility/resources, access/sharing/confidentiality and contextual six-/nine-mark reasoning.

Some of these already have questions elsewhere in the bank; they have not yet received this four-step treatment or a complete statement-level completeness audit. The next content pass should record each remaining statement individually, its existing evidence and its missing assessment representation, rather than inflating counts around already covered ideas.

## Editorial checks

- Foundation questions lower the retrieval burden, not the scientific standard.
- Diagnostic distractors target a specific confusion; feedback corrects it with a reason.
- Higher-demand tasks use mechanistic prediction, contextual explanation or evidence interpretation; unfamiliar necessary facts are supplied.
- No challenge is labelled as an official OCR question or assigned an invented six-/nine-mark score.
- Repetition and guided success do not establish retention.
- OCR samples informed response demands; no unreviewed examiner-report frequency claims are made. See [exam evidence](HUMAN_BIOLOGY_EXAM_EVIDENCE.md).
