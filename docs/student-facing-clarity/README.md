## Review against the criteria

- **Feedback.** On the element and energy-store reveals, the first visible feedback is the explanation. The definition or the worked matches and their reasoning appear before optional lesson notes. **Try a quick check** leads to the same scheduled item. The marked option or diagram remains available. Keyboard tests distinguish independent, hinted, corrected and revealed evidence and check that written self-review keeps the original response and revision.
- **Teaching.** The synapse headings make the causal sequence scannable without hiding it. F170 keeps the representations table with microscopy and labels the transition to fractionation. The F170 micrograph and A-level osmosis graph retain their original captions, scientific information and worked stages. Both sequences still lead to independent practice.
- **Navigation and footer.** The osmosis worked-example action and the synapse question action take precedence over optional support. Skills links keep their original labels, routes and touch targets. The Foundation and Higher inventory matched baseline across 60 paper and topic states. The footer keeps the published copyright markup, version, attribution and qualification disclaimer. Privacy is now linked alongside Terms of Use on both AQA app footers.
