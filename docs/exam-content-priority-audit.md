# Examined content and independent application: priority audit

Baseline: main `52e619c`, including PRs #82 and #83. This is a targeted audit of consequential examples, not a statement-by-statement certification of the three qualifications. The priorities concern what the resources offer; they do not establish what individual pupils have learned. Existing question counts and unresolved register rows are not evidence by themselves of missing teaching.

## Specification and recent work

- [AQA A-level Biology 7402](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/specification-at-a-glance) places comprehension in Paper 2 and experimental analysis and an essay in Paper 3. [The gene-expression specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/subject-content/the-control-of-gene-expression-a-level-only) requires interpretation of investigations. The recent [independent reasoning audit](alevel-independent-reasoning.md) and PR #83 already provide extensive paper-specific worked and independent practice; they should not be duplicated.
- [OCR Human Biology H049/H149 version 8](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf) specifies F170 and F171 in sections 5.2.1 and 5.2.2, including breadth and depth. The [October 2026 update](https://www.ocr.org.uk/administration/support-and-tools/subject-updates/can-human-biology-spec-update-764886/) clarifies recognition of reproductive structures in photomicrographs and corrects a fungal name. Both columns of content need attention.
- [AQA Trilogy 8464](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/specification-at-a-glance) assesses knowledge, application and evaluation. Its [assessment scheme](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/scheme-of-assessment) weights these approximately 40%, 40% and 20% respectively. Both Foundation and Higher pupils need opportunities to interpret unfamiliar evidence.

Other subsequent priorities include Higher motor-effect transfer, and GCSE practical reasoning about electrical energy loss, wire temperature and displacement. Batch 19 already introduced limiting-reactant teaching: the older Batch 18 register alone should not be used to claim it is absent. F170 endosymbiosis evidence also warrants a focused check against the full specification and current resources.

**Cross-cutting teaching access:** The GCSE revision renderer currently enters questions directly; `showIntro()` defines the key-idea screen but has no caller. Key text present in the source therefore cannot be counted as explicitly presented teaching in this route. This batch makes the food-test teaching visible at the point of use; other topics need a separate review of the rendered experience and of alternative catch-up teaching before changing site-wide behaviour. The A-level revision entry also goes directly to questions for ordinary topics, although its back control can reach the introduction and separate catch-up lessons provide teaching. The findings above distinguish source text, question feedback and rendered teaching where checked.

## Changes in this batch

The existing GCSE food-tests topic now distinguishes reducing from non-reducing sugars, describes an ethanol emulsion test, and models a positive and negative control before guided and independent interpretation. The independent responses use the existing self-review format. Existing questions retain their source order; the lesson renderer ranks questions by type, with the written applications presented after the supported check. The [mobile teaching screenshot](exam-content-review/food-tests-mobile.png) shows the new text for teacher review and contains no pupil work. The contraception topic also now distinguishes the combined pill, implant, copper IUD and hormonal IUS and qualifies STI protection. These distinctions follow the NHS explanations of the [combined pill](https://www.nhs.uk/contraception/methods-of-contraception/combined-pill/what-is-it/) and [copper IUD](https://www.nhs.uk/contraception/methods-of-contraception/iud-coil/what-is-it/).

The existing F170 microscopy lesson now traces the supernatant and pellets through successive spins, distinguishes enrichment from purity and links to supported and independent practice. The independent task asks for a written explanation and criteria-based self-review. It does not assign a correctness score to prose.

The A-level epigenetics revision wording now qualifies the role of promoter methylation and environmental influences, consistent with the existing more detailed `regulation` teaching. A chi-squared mnemonic no longer claims statistical significance proves chance played no part. These are accuracy corrections, not a replacement for the proposed gene-expression investigation above.

## Evidence of learning and limits

The changes provide opportunities for pupils to explain their own reasoning and compare it with criteria; software cannot establish the correctness of open prose. A teacher can compare a first unaided response with a later unseen problem, recording help and revisions separately. Check the scientific links and limitations, not completion counts. Browser and static checks establish technical behaviour, not pupil attainment. The remaining priorities are a sampled, ordered starting point, not a claim of complete curriculum coverage.
