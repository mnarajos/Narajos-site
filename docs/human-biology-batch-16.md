The 807 statement-level rows include 226 F170 and 148 F171 entries. The baseline is 67 Verified, 13 Taught, 32 Practised, 10 Represented, 3 Gap, 187 Review, 485 Unresolved and 10 Centre-only. Review and Unresolved are evidence states.

The three Gap rows were checked against the live content sources, from catch-up notes and guided lessons through the base bank, extension, rotating families and progressions. F170 1.1.2 names a haemocytometer in the underlying notes and F173 matching identifies its use. The comparative method and independent selection are missing. F171 1.2.1 covers airway damage and has cancer treatment and kidney examples. Neither hiatus hernia nor Hodgkin lymphoma has a specific learning sequence or independent causal question. OCR expects advantages and disadvantages of the two F170 counting methods, and physiological and functional consequences, signs, symptoms and impacts at F171 1.2.1. This F171 section excludes detailed cellular changes.

After addressing the three gaps, audit the Critical rows and a selection of High rows covering cell biology, microscopy, microbiology, physiological coordination, named diseases and diagnostic methods. Inspect actual explanations, the expected reasoning and distractor feedback before changing a row. An unchanged row can still have an audit decision where the evidence is too broad or a candidate is rejected. The review is bounded by the depth that can be checked reliably.
