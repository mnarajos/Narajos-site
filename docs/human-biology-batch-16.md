## Scientific changes

| Requirement | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F170 1.1.2-r12, counting methods | The microscopy notes named the haemocytometer. An F173 item identified its use. Neither explained the choice between manual observation and electrical counting. | Work through a defined-volume grid and compare it with counting impedance pulses. In the independent task, select a haemocytometer because cell fragments overlap whole cells in size but can be distinguished visually. Feedback separates a pulse count from cell identity and explains why an ordinary slide count lacks a volume denominator. |
| F171 1.2.1-r08, hiatus hernia | The cause-and-effect recap asked pupils to link tissue change to function but offered no hiatus hernia example. | An original labelled cross-section compares the usual junction position with a sliding hernia. The learner links displacement to a weaker barrier and possible reflux, then interprets fictional imaging and symptom findings. Feedback distinguishes reflux from increased secretion, sensitivity and delayed emptying. |
| F171 1.2.1-r13, Hodgkin lymphoma | Cancer questions compared treatments. They supplied no disease-specific causal model for enlarged nodes. | A worked findings table connects enlargement of lymphatic tissue to local pressure and symptoms, and distinguishes those observations from tissue diagnosis. Independent practice tests pressure on the oesophagus in a fictional case. The guided lesson also explains how a different anatomical site can affect drainage. |

The additional guided routes are F170 Gram envelopes and F171 polycystic kidney disease. The former compares envelope layers and uses a changed sample to infer stain colour. The latter separates urine output from filtration and reuses the existing kidney progression for independent written reasoning.

F170 explanations now distinguish nuclear, mitochondrial, ER and lysosomal roles, separate preparation choices for light microscopy, clarify valid peak-flow readings, contrast the two diabetes mechanisms and connect saltatory conduction to CNS demyelination. F171's COPD lesson includes functional consequences, clinical findings and daily impact. The existing F171 diagnostic techniques lesson now covers the four examination methods, initial observations, systolic and diastolic readings, a worked BMI calculation and a five-investigation evidence and limitations table. The unchanged respiratory case remains available.

The question bank goes from 569 to 577. Three questions are scoped to the three gap lessons. The five further applications cover imported mitochondrial proteins, epithelial surface area, slide preparation and thin sectioning, BMI interpretation and selecting evidence to characterise tissue. The existing guided, independent, revealed and self-reviewed states retain their distinct meanings. Earlier question and lesson identifiers retain their answer meanings. There are 54 guided routes and 49 Build Understanding progressions.

## Scientific and technical verification

- Mapped each reviewed requirement to the applicable OCR heading. Checked organelle roles against [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells) and [Cooper on mitochondrial protein import](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9896/), and envelope interpretation against [OpenStax Microbiology](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/4-4-gram-positive-bacteria). - Checked the disease mechanisms, symptoms and qualification of clinical inferences against the [NHS on hiatus hernia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hiatus-hernia/), the [clinical overview of sliding hernias](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562200/), [NCI on Hodgkin lymphoma](https://www.cancer.gov/types/lymphoma/patient/adult-hodgkin-treatment-pdq), [NHS COPD](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/chronic-obstructive-pulmonary-disease-copd/), [NHS ADPKD](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/autosomal-dominant-polycystic-kidney-disease-adpkd/symptoms/) and [NHS multiple sclerosis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/multiple-sclerosis/). Check infectious-disease signs and symptom limitations separately.
