# Batch 21 physiological disease evidence

Baseline 4037df7 had passing site and browser checks and no outstanding pull requests. The October 2026 version 8 specification (F171 pp. 50–51), Batch 16 decisions, subtopic notes, focused lessons, progressions and practice bank were checked together.

COPD, hiatus hernia, polycystic kidney disease and Hodgkin lymphoma already had explicit structural models and application. Their original routes and assessment records are retained. MND, abdominal aortic aneurysm, Crohn’s disease, nephritis, iron deficiency, cystic fibrosis, sickle cell anaemia and melanoma lacked comparable disease-specific evidence in the register. Parkinson’s, hypertension, asthma, MS, rheumatoid arthritis and rickets had examples or risk-factor references, but these did not establish a complete explanation from altered physiology to daily function.

Reference: [OCR specification](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf).

Seven Area 1 comparison routes add an independent written case after worked reasoning and a diagnostic choice. These use local self-review with question-specific criteria. The abdominal aorta case uses two labelled schematics and tests whether pupils confuse diameter with pressure. The renal case compares barrier leakage with loss of waste clearance. The movement case shifts from grip and initiation to speech and swallowing. Exact routes and cases are recorded in the F171 register.

Across 148 F171 rows, Verified rises from 24 to 28 and Taught from 4 to 21. The four new Verified decisions cover MND, abdominal aortic aneurysm, nephritis and the section 1.1 sign/symptom distinction. Fourteen named conditions were given stronger or newly indexed teaching; the four established Verified examples retain their status. The shared oxygen-delivery case does not independently assess CF inheritance. Melanoma, rheumatoid arthritis, Parkinson’s disease, CF digestive effects, and wider societal effects merit more independent application. Section 1.1 definitions outside the affected sign/symptom distinction remain open. No observed pupil trial was conducted.

Scientific cross-checks included NHS descriptions of [MND](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/motor-neurone-disease/), [Parkinson’s](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/parkinsons-disease/), [abdominal aortic aneurysm](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/abdominal-aortic-aneurysm/), [glomerulonephritis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/glomerulonephritis/) and [cystic fibrosis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/cystic-fibrosis/).
