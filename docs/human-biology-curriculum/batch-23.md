# F170 foundations and interpretation of evidence

Batch 22 merged in PR 74 at e33106d. The site and browser workflows both passed on that main commit. The starting register contained 37 Verified and seven Taught rows in F170. This audit uses the named examples and exclusions in OCR Version 8, pp. 33–37.

| Starting point | What a student could already do | Teaching or assessment needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1.1 blood | Recall why a mature erythrocyte lacks a nucleus and measure a real erythrocyte elsewhere in microscopy. | Distinguish oxygen carriage from white-cell functions, and use structural evidence to predict a functional loss. |
| 1.1.1 neurones, rods and cones | Follow the existing reflex diagram from receptor to effector. | Link cell shape to local integration or long-distance transmission. Explain a change in vision using light and colour evidence. |
| 1.1.1 hepatocytes, renal, ciliated, squamous and gastric pit epithelium | Apply microvillar surface area and airway clearance. Alveolar and nephron routes already exist. | Compare the site of transport, secretion or movement in different tissues. Include the surface mucous cells of gastric pits. |
| 1.1.1 muscles and gametes | Connect cardiac contraction to pressure. Distinguish the existing ovarian and testicular micrographs by tissue organisation. | Explain differences between muscle types and between the two gametes. Tissue identification alone cannot demonstrate either skill. |
| 1.1.3 organelles | Trace collagen through RER, Golgi and secretory vesicles. Four rows were Taught. | Use different evidence to reason about ribosome assembly and location, ER specialisation and delivery to lysosomes. |
| 1.1.4 membrane | Explain osmosis and decide whether uptake needs energy. | Place components on the bilayer and interpret evidence about stability, signalling, recognition and vesicular transport. |
| 1.1.5 and microscopy | Use a scale bar, distinguish alignment from separation in text and reason about chromosome allocation. | Read unfamiliar image evidence, count chromosomes separately from DNA molecules and compare the two meiotic divisions with mitosis. |

The original reflex, tissue and transport models, licensed reproductive micrographs, Skills Practice, rotating question families and Build Understanding progressions provide starting points for these decisions.
