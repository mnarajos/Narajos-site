## Teaching and independent application

| Example | What was available before | What a pupil does now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specialised surfaces | A question compared flat cells with cells bearing microvilli. | In a kidney sample glucose recovery falls while the brush border remains intact. The pupil uses ATP and the ion gradient to explain the result. A paired liver sample requires different reasoning about protein production and export. |
| Organelles | The worked collagen question tested where a protein accumulated on the way to secretion. | In a separate case, digestive enzymes arrive in the lysosome in normal amounts but its interior becomes less acidic. The pupil uses both observations to explain why material remains. ER drawings and a pancreatic transmission electron micrograph then test which structural evidence supports protein synthesis and what a still image can establish. |
| Membranes | The existing independent task predicted volume change using water potential. | The new comparison uses tracer leakage and lateral lipid mobility in two model endothelial membranes at the same temperature. Both results are needed to explain a benefit and a possible cost of the higher cholesterol content. |
| Cell division | Pupils could identify metaphase from a verbal description of equatorial alignment. | Pupils trace a chromatid in a real human chromosome SEM, justify an interpretation of a different onion-root LM field, and decide whether a neighbouring cell's appearance establishes S phase. A separate meiotic problem asks what separates and counts DNA molecules at one pole. |

In written review the evidence stays visible beside the original attempt, criteria and revision.
