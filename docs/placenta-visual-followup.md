# Placenta visual: follow-up design

The F175/a2 teaching text and worked steps remain available in Phase 1, but the single-villus exchange figure and the separate many-villi schematic are withheld from the live explanation, worked stages and recaps. Their editable SVG files and accompanying source modules are retained. Phase 2 replaces them with an original pregnancy-orientation view and a linked enlarged-villus view; the earlier files remain withheld. The review criteria below document the basis for that replacement.

The replacement should begin with an orientation view showing the pregnant person, uterus, placenta, umbilical cord and foetus. Clearly distinguish the maternal and foetal sides before zooming in to a villus and its foetal capillaries. Keep each zoom connected to the preceding view, and explain that sizes and distances are simplified rather than implying a real scale.

Use a consistent legend to distinguish label leaders from arrows showing net gas transfer. Label oxygen and carbon dioxide, show their directions across the placental barrier, and explain the arrow convention and any visual exaggeration. Do not suggest that maternal and foetal blood normally mix or that the umbilical cord carries maternal blood. Keep British spellings, including “foetus” and “foetal”, in visible labels, captions and alternatives.

Before a later release, check the new representations and captions against the OCR Human Biology requirements and authoritative anatomy and physiology sources. Review them at phone and desktop sizes with their exact explanation and worked stage, provide an accessible text explanation, and verify keyboard and touch behaviour, independent-assessment isolation and progress records. Record the specific evidence and any remaining limits in the [Human Biology block ledger](catchup-visual-coverage-human-steps.md).
