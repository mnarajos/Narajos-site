# AQA 7402 assessment and terminology evidence

Checked 25 November 2026. The intended curriculum comes from the current specification. The assessed curriculum includes what a question asks students to recall or derive, what its stem supplies, and what its mark scheme credits for that particular task. The enacted curriculum is the language and reasoning Narajos teaches and assesses. Enrichment can add scientifically useful detail where the core explanation and exam expectations are clear. ## Assessment sample and implications for teaching

The sample covered the original question papers, mark schemes and examiner reports for all three papers in June 2022–2025, with earlier June 2018 and 2019 papers used for targeted terminology checks. The examples below are paraphrased. The lessons use new contexts and independently written questions. [AQA assessment resources][resources]

| Skill | What the paper and report show | Teaching application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify the entity and endpoint | June 2024 Paper 1 Q02.3 asked about intact vitamin A reaching cells. Some answers focused on lipid digestion or continued into events after absorption. Q09.3 asked students to reason from two tables, and some read time in courtship as the number of females in courtship. [Question paper][24p1qp] · [Mark scheme][24p1ms] · [Examiner report][24p1er] | Identify the entity, the quantity and the endpoint before planning an answer. Use the table heading and units to check what the evidence represents. |
| Respond to the command | June 2023 Paper 1 Q05.1 asked for a description, so lengthy explanations earned no additional credit. In Q09.4 most responses to an evaluation examined only one side of the question. [Question paper][23p1qp] · [Mark scheme][23p1ms] · [Examiner report][23p1er] | Contrast what a description, an explanation and an evaluation each need to achieve. Practise answering a different command about the same stimulus. |
| Choose and apply relevant information | In June 2025 Paper 2 Q02.5, full-credit responses used the supplied figure to describe and explain changes to blood pH and ventilation during exercise. Responses about heart rate addressed a neighbouring outcome. [Question paper][25p2qp] · [Mark scheme][25p2ms] · [Examiner report][25p2er] | Select the observation for each named variable and connect it to relevant biological knowledge. |
| Obey restrictions and conditions | June 2024 Paper 3 Q02.1 requested additional controls. Candidates sometimes repeated controls already present or named generic variables without applying them to the investigation. In June 2025 Paper 3 Q04.4, some ignored explicit exclusions or proposed future experiments instead of explaining the scientists’ decision. [2024 question paper][24p3qp] · [2024 mark scheme][24p3ms] · [2024 examiner report][24p3er] · [2025 question paper][25p3qp] · [2025 mark scheme][25p3ms] · [2025 examiner report][25p3er] | Identify both the requested output and the boundary on acceptable responses. Check what the method already controls before suggesting a change. |
| Complete a causal explanation | June 2023 Paper 1 Q10.3 answers sometimes described key events in meiosis and stopped before explaining how these produced new combinations of alleles. June 2024 Paper 1 Q08.1 responses often named elastic tissue without linking stretch and recoil to the maintenance of pressure. [2023 question paper][23p1qp] · [2023 mark scheme][23p1ms] · [2023 examiner report][23p1er] · [2024 question paper][24p1qp] · [2024 mark scheme][24p1ms] · [2024 examiner report][24p1er] | Show how each intermediate step leads to the outcome named in the question. Check whether the final sentence reaches that outcome. |
| Compare like with like | June 2024 Paper 1 Q06.1 requested structural differences between mRNA and tRNA. Some paired a statement about the strand count of one molecule with a statement about the shape of the other. [Question paper][24p1qp] · [Mark scheme][24p1ms] · [Examiner report][24p1er] | Use the same characteristic for both entities in each comparison. Name the two entities or make each reference unambiguous. |
| Meet combined demands | June 2024 Paper 1 Q08.1 paired identification of a structure with explanation of its function. June 2025 Paper 2 Q02.5 paired description with explanation using a figure. [2024 question paper][24p1qp] · [2024 mark scheme][24p1ms] · [2025 question paper][25p2qp] · [2025 examiner report][25p2er] | Give each demand a visible place in a draft and connect the response components. Use original free responses and criteria for independent transfer. |
| Distinguish evidence from inference | June 2024 Paper 3 Q06.7 reports that some students treated standard deviations as evidence of no significant difference while also correctly observing that a statistical test was missing. [Question paper][24p3qp] · [Mark scheme][24p3ms] · [Examiner report][24p3er] | Distinguish the spread of observations, a statistical test result and evidence about a causal mechanism. Treat unsupported explanations as possibilities. |

AQA’s assessment writers discuss distinguishing command words, combining commands, introducing unfamiliar terms with context and allowing genuinely equivalent wording. They describe points-based marking for questions other than the essay. This supports modelling in new contexts and using self-review criteria for original written work. ## Vocabulary decisions

| Candidate | Specification and assessment evidence | Narajos decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Interstitial fluid** | §3.3.4.1 uses **tissue fluid**. June 2023 Paper 1 Q09.6 uses that wording and credits an explanation about the water-potential difference after proteins move out of capillaries. The term *interstitial fluid* was absent from the documents searched. [Specification §3.3][exchange] · [Question paper][23p1qp] · [Mark scheme][23p1ms] | Default to **tissue fluid** for the circulation topic and present **interstitial fluid** as a synonym for interpretation. Explain the tissue and fluid compartments in context. The pre-batch teaching data used *interstitial fluid* in a collecting-duct question and the terminology bank accepted it for *tissue fluid*. |
| **Erythrocyte** | §3.3.4.1 uses **red blood cells**. June 2019 Paper 2 Q02 introduces sickle cell disease using **red blood cells** in the stimulus. The exact word *erythrocyte* was absent from the documents searched. [Specification §3.3][exchange] · [Question paper][19p2qp] | Default to **red blood cell**, with **erythrocyte** as an interpretive synonym worth retaining in biological drawing. A mature human red blood cell lacks a nucleus. A frog red blood cell has a nucleus, as the drawing exercise shows. |
| **Oncotic pressure** | §3.3.4.1 specifies tissue-fluid formation and return. June 2023 Paper 1 Q09.6 accepts water-potential reasoning in the stated case. *Oncotic pressure* was absent from the documents searched and the baseline A-level teaching. [Specification §3.3][exchange] · [Mark scheme][23p1ms] · [Microvascular fluid exchange][starling] | Optional physiology enrichment. Introduce it as osmotic pressure due to proteins when it adds explanatory value. For the core lesson, describe outward filtration, the effect of retained plasma proteins and lymphatic return. Label the venous capillary return model by its assumptions and original question context. |
| **Endothelium** | §3.3.4.1 specifies vessel structure and function. June 2024 Paper 1 Q08.1 explicitly credits reduced friction linked to the smooth endothelium of the aorta. June 2019 Paper 3 Q01.1 accepts *epithelium* for *endothelium* in its specific glomerular filtration mark point. [Specification §3.3][exchange] · [2024 mark scheme][24p1ms] · [2024 examiner report][24p1er] · [2019 mark scheme][19p3ms] | Teach **endothelium** as the cellular lining of a blood vessel. Retain it in vessel structure and gas exchange, with a short explanation on first use. The 2024 question gives one credited structure–function example among several. |
| **Gluconeogenesis** | §3.6.4.2 names gluconeogenesis and the liver’s role. June 2019 Paper 2 Q05.1 requires knowledge of glucagon’s role in this process. June 2025 Paper 2 Q02.3 expects students to recognise it as a result of glucagon action. [Specification §3.6][respond] · [2019 question paper][19p2qp] · [2019 mark scheme][19p2ms] · [2025 question paper][25p2qp] · [2025 mark scheme][25p2ms] | Teach **gluconeogenesis** as core, using examples such as glycerol, lactate and some amino acids. Distinguish it from glycogen breakdown. A glucagon explanation should identify enzyme activation and glucose formation from non-carbohydrate precursors. |
| **Troponin** | §3.6.3 explicitly exempts the role of troponin. June 2019 Paper 2 Q04.2 ignores it for the relevant mark point. The June 2019 Paper 3 examiner report says a bare mention of the word did not constitute material beyond the specification in that essay. [Specification §3.6][respond] · [2019 Paper 2 mark scheme][19p2ms] · [2019 Paper 3 examiner report][19p3er] | Class it as enrichment where useful for explaining the mechanism. Model the core sequence with calcium ions, tropomyosin movement, exposed actin binding sites and actin–myosin cross-bridge formation. |
| **Allosteric regulation** | §3.1.4.2 specifies competitive and non-competitive inhibition. June 2023 Paper 1 Q08.2 accepts **allosteric site** as an alternative to describing a binding site distinct from the active site. That mark point assesses the binding location. [Specification §3.1][molecules] · [2023 mark scheme][23p1ms] · [2023 examiner report][23p1er] · [IUPAC allosteric enzymes][allostery] | Teach the binding location and its effect in the example. **Allosteric site** can be recognised and used as a shortcut where accurate. **Allosteric regulation** is broader and can include activation as well as inhibition. |

At the start of the batch, the main topic lessons and vocabulary bank practised gluconeogenesis and used endothelium in gas exchange and vessel structure. The drawing examples used erythrocyte, the terminology bank paired interstitial fluid with tissue fluid, and a collecting-duct question used interstitial fluid without a gloss. The enzyme lesson and vocabulary bank asked students to name allosteric site. Troponin appeared in both an enrichment lesson and an essay model. These uses informed where to retain specialist terms, gloss them, accept equivalent wording or label enrichment.

An additional wording decision concerns insulin-stimulated glucose uptake. The current specification refers to **channel proteins** in its insulin wording. June 2019 Paper 2 Q05.3 explicitly accepts **carrier proteins** or **GLUT4** for that point. GLUT4 is a glucose carrier that mediates facilitated diffusion. Specify insulin-responsive tissues when teaching its recruitment to the membrane. [Specification §3.6][respond] · [2019 mark scheme][19p2ms] · [2019 examiner report][19p2er] · [Human GLUT4 structure and transport][glut4]

An exact-term search across the sample can show what was found in those documents. It cannot determine what an examiner will accept for a different future question. The individual mark schemes themselves make the same distinction. [2024 Paper 1 mark scheme, introductory guidance][24p1ms]
