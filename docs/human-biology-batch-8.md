## Specification reconciliation

- F170 3.3.1 explicitly includes recognising uterus, ovarian tissue and testes in photomicrographs. Detailed menstrual-cycle content is excluded from 3.3.1. The F170 hormonal pathway belongs with 3.3.2, with extended cycle context clearly identified where given.
- F170 4.1.2 already explains the corrected term for athlete's foot, tinea pedis, and distinguishes the infection from the causative fungi.
- F175's corrected P1 reference is **3.4 Reproductive health plan**. This is an assessment-reference correction. The app provides general reproduction knowledge and original independent exercises. The F175 assessment guidance and task are outside the teaching materials.
- F170 and F171 are externally assessed. F172–F177 use original general knowledge for NEA units.

## Implementation

The batch adds 39 guided lessons across 41 routes. The F171 treatment and monitoring overviews share their corresponding section lessons and saved responses. The catalogue labels guided lessons and shorter recaps. It retains existing note routes and the established question-bank entry points.

| Unit | Guided teaching |
| --- | --- |
| F170 | Measure a red cell with its scale bar, compare imaging methods and units, reason from a membrane gradient, trace damage from tissue to system, follow oxygen and blood flow, calculate ventilation, work through blood glucose feedback and a reflex, recognise three reproductive tissues, and connect FSH, LH and sex steroids with their effects. |
| F171 | Compare immobilisation with fixation and apply that reasoning to other treatments. Distinguish prevention, screening, diagnosis and monitoring. Classify results from a screening table, interpret fictional repeated readings and missing values, trace immune memory, compare vaccine types and immune dysfunction, and assess whether study results support causation. |
| F172 | Connect genes and chromosomes to cell division, work through a Punnett square and conditional probability, interpret a limited genetic test and trace the intended effect of gene therapy. |
| F173 | Compare light microscopy, TEM and SEM, reuse the red-cell scale exercise, then use standards and controls to interpret biochemical results and chromatography. |
| F174 | Distinguish energy and nutrient intake, trace digestion and absorption into metabolism, and evaluate what BMI and repeated measurements can establish. |
| F175 | Model the two gamete routes, hormonal control, placental exchange, reduced fertility and assisted reproduction. |
| F176 | Trace sensory and motor pathways, model synaptic transmission and reduced inhibition, distinguish nociception and pain, and compare evidence about injury from EEG, MRI and CT. |
| F177 | Compare agonists and antagonists, uncontrolled and randomised evidence, and what sample composition, size and duration allow students to conclude about effectiveness and safety. |

The red-cell photograph, osmosis and exchange visuals, circulation assets, cardiac simulator, withdrawal reflex, and vaccine diagrams remain available with the relevant explanations. The F170 reproductive anatomy lesson adds three resized, locally hosted images. The F175 simulation is available within the worked example, before guided application. It has pause, resume, replay and reset controls. The event showing possible implantation is separate from the fertilisation and early-embryo animation. A written sequence and the simulation status provide alternatives to visual motion.

Skills links open the existing worked examples for the relevant method, with an explanation of what to practise and a route back to the lesson. Catch-up-linked Skills Practice has its own saved set per skill, so it can run alongside the unfinished question bank or flashcard session. Exam questions can link directly to the teaching. Existing sets, identifiers, scores and scheduling remain supported.

Guided tasks use graduated hints and option-specific feedback. Independent questions use new examples. Short written responses can be compared with separate scientific points and a model. Correct first responses, hint-supported responses, corrections, answers after seeing an explanation and independent follow-up responses have separate outcomes. Written self-review counts separately in the exam question engine. These outcomes represent practice evidence, and historical results are retained as recorded.

The NEA routes contain authored scientific explanations, fictional or unrelated examples, diagrams and question-specific self-review. They use no assignment templates, student submissions, uploads, writing tools, marking rubrics or advice tailored to a live task.

## Verification and limits

Browser coverage exercises guided and independent activities at all 41 routes, a separate independent follow-up, written drafts, selected-error feedback, the three tissue images, Skills Practice and flashcard return, keyboard controls, and both animated and reduced-motion reproduction paths. Width checks include 320, 390, 768 and 1280 pixels. Automated accessibility checks exercise open Human Biology teaching at narrow and desktop widths. The repository still runs the established static, flashcard, question-bank, Skills Practice, browser and accessibility checks.

The guided lessons cover selected priorities. The remaining catch-up routes offer short recaps and existing practice. Histology uses representative fields and is not a full specimen atlas. Physical mobile devices, specialist screen readers and pupil usability testing are outside the completed checks. Local WebKit is unavailable because the host lacks its graphics libraries. The repository browser workflow runs WebKit on its configured runner.
