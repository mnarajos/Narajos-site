# Plan an investigation: scientific and assessment review

This record supports review of the AQA A-level Biology investigation-planning sequence. The four briefs are original teaching tasks; they are not AQA examination questions or mark schemes. The activities teach planning, not laboratory competence. A correct structured choice or a completed revision is not evidence that an unrestricted written method would receive examination credit.

## Calibration

[AQA practical assessment](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/practical-assessment) includes practical problem solving and experimental design, and identifies investigations of solute concentration and membrane permeability among the required practicals. [AQA's assessment scheme](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/scheme-of-assessment) includes developing and refining procedures in AO3. Written assessment of practical skills and the practical endorsement are distinct.

The [June 2024 Paper 2 mark scheme, question 08.4](https://www.aqa.org.uk/files/sample-papers-and-mark-schemes.2024.June.AQA-74022-MS-JUN24_PDF), accepts several measurements and allocations in its particular growth-regulator question. It is useful evidence that a single expression or allocation should not be imposed on a different task. Credit in a particular examination question depends on its wording and mark scheme; a valid improvement to a design is not necessarily a required mark point. The seedling task uses a different original brief and individually treated pots.

## Scientific decisions to review

| Context | Intended inference and comparison | Key design relationships and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Potato and sucrose | Compare percentage mass changes of potato pieces after exposure to prepared sucrose concentrations. | Measure initial and final mass for each piece, with consistent exposure and blotting. Calculate each piece's change as a percentage of its own nonzero initial mass before averaging by treatment. Comparable material and independently treated vessels support the comparison. The task does not require dilution design or a water-potential estimate. |
| Beetroot and temperature | Compare pigment released into solution after equal periods at different temperatures. | Standardise tissue preparation, solution volume and time; rinse consistently. The colorimeter is zeroed with clear water; greater absorbance indicates more pigment in the solution under the stated procedure. The instrument blank is not an independent experimental treatment. |
| Growth regulator and seedling stems | The question is: “Does the concentration of a supplied growth regulator affect the increase in stem length of young seedlings over seven days?” Comparing the supplied 1 and 5 mg dm⁻³ treatments addresses differences across those tested concentrations; a matching-solvent zero treatment supports an additional comparison with no added regulator. | Record initial and final length and compare changes, or use another justified method. Percentage increase is relative to starting length and can yield a different pattern from absolute increase. Final lengths may support comparison if initial lengths are suitably standardised or accounted for. Change alone cannot remove allocation bias or other confounding. The number of independent treatment units is determined by how treatment is applied. |
| Cress and salt | Compare germination after five days in salt and zero-salt conditions under the supplied conditions. | Independently treated pots are treatment units. Compare counts or proportions of seeds germinated; proportions permit comparison of unequal groups. Keep light uniform or balance treatments across positions to avoid confounding. Conclusions apply to these seeds and conditions. A different outcome can be valid if its observations and processing support it. |

A shared treatment vessel is not automatically several independent treatment replicates, even if it contains several specimens. Repeated instrument readings do not create new specimens. Repetition or averaging cannot rescue an otherwise confounded comparison. Controls must be appropriate to the stated investigation; they are not interchangeable labels.

## Review protocol

Use the actual activity and its tests to record evidence against these cases before release:

- A pupil needing help can use the worked potato decision, see the whole plan and repair a specific problem. The beetroot task retains a familiar representation and available worked help without presupposing colorimetry.
- Each first-three plan connects a treatment comparison to measurements, timing, allocation, conditions and processing. Individually reasonable choices that conflict receive actionable feedback at the decision that needs changing.
- Feasible alternative allocations and defensible measurements are not rejected solely for differing from an example. Missing details and approaches outside authored checks are not described as scientifically wrong.
- An edited decision invalidates dependent success feedback. Rechecking evaluates the current plan; a corrected structured response is not described as an independently generated examination answer.
- In the transfer activity, the initial response is stored before checks appear. The initial response, follow-up decisions and revision remain distinguishable after reload. Follow-up correctness does not mark the original response. The revision prompts a concrete improvement or clarification, even if the original was sound.
- Word definitions and accessibility support remain available without implying that planning decisions were supplied. Learner-controlled help can be opened on a narrow screen and does not obstruct revision.
- Verify keyboard and touch flows, feedback announcement and focus, zoom and narrow layouts, local save/resume/reset and unavailable storage. Confirm that older A-level progress keys and identifiers remain intact.

For each release, attach the exact commit, test commands and outcomes, captures of desktop and phone journeys, and any unresolved accessibility or scientific-review findings. Automated checks and screenshots do not establish pupil attainment or replace a teacher's review of written work. For learning evidence, compare independently written responses to different unseen tasks before and after practice, recording support and revisions separately and accepting other defensible approaches.
