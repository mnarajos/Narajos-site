# Batch 18 baseline coverage register

Baseline: GitHub main at 71eade6. The [AQA 8464 specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification) (Version 1.3, October 2026) is the reference. Topic codes in this register link to GCSE revision, where notes, question sets, Foundation/Higher filters and worked feedback are available. Catch-up entries are identified by the topic parameter in `/science-catch-up/lesson/?topic=…`. C4 and B5 have entry points in both senior catalogues and retain one source and progress key.

There are 42 distinct guided routes in Years 9–11. Year 9 has 6 Biology, 4 Chemistry and 2 Physics. Year 10 has 9 Biology, 4 Chemistry and 6 Physics. Year 11 has 2 Biology, 5 Chemistry and 4 Physics.
