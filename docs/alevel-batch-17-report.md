# A-level Biology Batch 17

## What changed

Five lessons in the existing Exam Skills catalogue add 18 original tasks. Each opens with a principle and a worked sequence, then moves from supported practice to independent choice, calculation or written application. The [editorial register](alevel-batch-17-audit.md) records what already existed, the examination evidence, the priorities and the validation criteria.

| Lesson | Worked reasoning and transfer |
| --- | --- |
| [Understand the question](https://narajos.com/alevel/#exam-interpret) | Identify the target, use only relevant conditions and answer each part of a dual demand. Model absorption of an intact lipid-soluble molecule. Transfer to mitosis, ADH, interpretation of the unchanged mRNA result and a solvent-control explanation. |
| [Reason through practicals](https://narajos.com/alevel/#exam-practical) | Use a beetroot data table to connect a confound to its effect. Separate a fixed endpoint from a fixed observation time. Test outcome-independent field selection, the limitations of matching chromatogram spots, and evaluation of a novel respirometer comparison. Compare validity, precision, accuracy, repeatability, reproducibility, error and uncertainty in their appropriate contexts. |
| [Explain precisely](https://narajos.com/alevel/#exam-precision) | Trace sodium ion movement and membrane potential from two meaning-equivalent descriptions. Distinguish signed water potentials, sister chromatids and homologues, transcription and translation, coding and amino-acid incorporation, and cardiac pressure differences. Written review allows the pupil to see the first draft, model criteria and revised response. |
| [Interpret data](https://narajos.com/alevel/#exam-evidence) | Select elapsed time and change in a cumulative measurement. Distinguish a plateau from its possible causes. Interpolate an RP11-style glucose calibration and give targeted feedback on the denominator and units for mass loss. Interpret a non-significant result and evaluate a fictional downstream field dataset. |
| [Develop extended answers](https://narajos.com/alevel/#exam-extended) | Relate cardiac output, muscle blood flow and arterial oxygen content to delivery. Compare paragraph endings for an essay title. Explain a plant response using gas exchange, photosynthesis and transport, then qualify the claim in light of other effects of dry soil. The dedicated Paper 3 essay practice is available from the catalogue. |

The assessment evidence covers representative 7402 Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 demands. The original tasks use fictional data and wording. The exam-board evidence justifies the type of thinking required. It does not make these official AQA questions or imply that one model wording is required.

## Examples before and after

| Priority | Existing task | Batch 17 addition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question interpretation | The countercurrent lesson gave the conditions and asked what follows in a novel exchanger. Pupils applied the mechanism once the relationship was identified. | The ADH task gives equal hormone concentrations and equal water-potential differences. Pupils must identify aquaporin insertion as the differing step and resist importing the usual explanation about altered ADH release. |
| Practical reasoning | The practical-methods lesson included matching a step to a reason, such as letting a respirometer equilibrate to stabilise temperature and pressure. | The new written comparison gives dormant and germinating seeds at different temperatures. Pupils must explain why temperature undermines the causal claim and justify holding it constant. The seeds are a transfer context for the respirometry principle. |
| Scientific precision | The mechanisms lesson asks which explanation describes how insulin lowers blood glucose. | The new model compares two scientifically equivalent ways to describe sodium ion movement, distinguishes ion entry from firing an action potential and then asks pupils to write the pressure comparison that explains delayed aortic-valve opening. |

## Integration and verification

The existing 52 tasks and 56 drawing variants were preserved. Topic links and the topic finder use the same Exam Skills catalogue. Targeted offers after incorrect or revealed responses in 15 existing topic lessons open the skill lesson alongside the original question. Closing it returns focus to the offer and preserves the response and feedback. Links from Exam Skills to related Catch-up topics retain the existing return route. The question banks, Maths Skills generators, catch-up, flashcards, essay modules and stored progress schema were preserved.

Incorrect choice feedback is matched to the authored answer identity after display order changes. The incorrect submission and its feedback survive reload. Independent correctness, supported performance, correction, revealed explanations and written self-review retain their existing meanings. Writing earns no automated score and records self-review only when the pupil selects Finish review.

Local Chromium ran the full A-level Exam Skills suite. It exercised all 70 authored tasks and 56 drawing judgements, both Core modes, paper navigation, practicals, Maths Skills, flashcards and spaced review, focus and keyboard, touch-emulated navigation, before-and-after drafts, contextual return, legacy outcome restoration and resizing. Axe checks sampled the existing and new routes at 320, 390, 768 and 1280 pixels. The suite also checks enlarged text, reduced motion and 2× browser viewport zoom in representative journeys. The existing GitHub workflows provide wider site and browser-engine coverage.

Pupils, physical devices and specialist assistive technology were not tested. Automated browser success establishes that the journeys work, with learning impact still to be evaluated. Centre Services material and unverified recent examiner reports were outside the evidence sample.

## Remaining priorities

1. Observe sixth-form pupils interpret and answer a fresh question after using the new lesson. Compare relevance, reasoning and use of evidence with a first independent attempt.
2. Review legacy statistical wording in the older Paper 3 lessons and Maths Skills generators. In particular, scope SD-bar inference to the question and revise wording that treats a non-significant test as accepting the null hypothesis. The new data lesson teaches the conditional interpretation explicitly.
3. If pupil work indicates a need, add further transfer on microscopy calibration, log-axis interpretation and method selection in the remainder of the required practicals. Base any additions on a gap in reasoning practice.
4. Continue Batch 18 as the separate Year 10–11 Catch-up project.
