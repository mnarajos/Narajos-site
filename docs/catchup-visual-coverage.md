# Catch-up visual coverage

This is a working inventory, **not a claim of full visual coverage**. The step ledgers record the exact explanations assessed and the visuals visible beside them, with separate assessments of accuracy, teaching relevance and rendered readability. “Pending”, “partial” and unlisted stages remain open. A route count, visual mapping or automated presence check does not establish meaningful coverage. Independent checks should not reveal lesson answers.

The concept inventories below are an index and do not replace the block ledgers. All checked claims in a ledger have the scope stated there; a visual may cover only a part of its explanation.

## A-level Biology

The catch-up index has 171 concepts across topics 1–8 (17, 20, 23, 23, 22, 28, 21 and 17). The shared registry has 20 diagram/sequence mappings and one simulation-only mapping. Eight guided routes have additional models. Neither mapping nor a guided route establishes coverage of all key passages. For instance, the shared enzyme graph does not explain enzyme structure or inhibition, and the osmosis figure does not explain membrane structure. See [the detailed concept inventory](catchup-visual-coverage-alevel.md) for passage counts and mapping status. New original figures and a locally credited micrograph support selected paired-passage groups. The block ledger records their precise scope and outstanding scientific and rendered review.

## AAQ Human Biology

The companion index contains 90 routes: 37 F170, 29 F171, and 4, 3, 4, 5, 5 and 3 across F172–F177. These include F170 sections, F171–F177 notes and focused lessons. See [the route inventory](catchup-visual-coverage-human.md). F171–F177 notes contain 38 routes and 109 key-idea lines; some resolve to guided lessons instead of short recaps. The shared visual registry contains 15 Human Biology keys, 14 of which specify a diagram, sequence or microscope visual. The remainder links to a simulation. In the existing short recap renderer the visual is inserted on step 0 only. Subsequent key ideas, guided teaching stages, and focused lessons need separate visual review. Selected original diagrams appear in explanation and worked-model stages. The block ledger records the specific stages and known gaps. These counts do not include all visuals in teaching data and are not coverage totals.
