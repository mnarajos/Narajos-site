# A-level Biology Batch 9

## Scope

Batch 9 starts from the verified Batch 8 commit. It improves the Batch 7 A-level resources across ten teaching families. The 52 reasoning tasks and 56 drawing variants remain available. Work is confined to the A-level experience and a backwards-compatible option in the shared topic finder.

## Reliability and progress

| Before | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Feedback could refer to the author's answer order after choices had been shuffled. | Feedback refers to the scientific claim. The same diffusion answer receives the same explanation in any position. |
| A refreshed activity could show an answer as selected but require it to be checked again. | The selected answer, result, feedback, hints, attempts and position restore together. The appropriate continuation button appears immediately. |
| A repeat submission could change an already accepted outcome. | Accepted work is read-only. Only an explicit retry starts a new attempt. A targeted retry leaves independently correct questions and their outcomes in place. |
| Opening criteria recorded a written answer as self-reviewed. | The draft remains editable during review. Each criterion can be marked Included or Needs work, and both the judgements and revised draft are saved. Finish review records a self-assessment. Legacy checklist-open records resume as pending reviews. |
| Completion could lead to a generic message. | Review shows the actual outcomes, including independent, guided, supported, corrected, written self-review and tasks still needing practice. Students can inspect the completed questions or retry the tasks that needed help. |

Model answers and written criteria support judgement without assigning an automated prose mark. The review reports evidence of practice.

## Learning flow

The current stage is marked in a compact sequence from Key idea through Worked example, With support, Your turn and Review. A filled button moves the student to the next useful step. Previous, Replay, optional help and navigation use quieter controls. The next action follows the feedback. Moving into practice closes the example and focuses the current question. Students can reopen the example at their saved step or go directly to independent questions from the start.

Blue identifies worked examples, violet identifies support, and teal identifies independent work. Stage labels and functional icons supplement the colours. Current tasks use icons for writing, calculations, grouping or diagrams. Correctness, clues, revision and revealed explanations each have visible labels.

## Activities and navigation

- The drawing task keeps both images next to each other at larger widths. On phones the student can switch between the reference and candidate without scrolling between them. Both can be enlarged. Requirement bullets sit by the figures. Reject reveals the category controls, and submitted judgements can show dashed annotations over the relevant drawing features.
- The dihybrid example reveals parental genotypes, then gametes, grid headings, one combination, the completed grid and phenotype interpretation. Its ratios are conditional on the stated inheritance assumptions.
- The cardiac schematic highlights the relevant pair of pressure traces and the calculated crossing. Countercurrent exchange highlights neighbouring values at the same physical position before comparing the parallel model.
- The disinhibition example highlights individual links. The enzyme case identifies the reversible inhibitor, enzyme, substrate and product. The neural case distinguishes reduced inhibition from reaching threshold.
- Grouping questions preserve correct assignments and flag only unresolved rows. Hints point to the relevant comparison, molecule, criterion or causal link. A selected answer has a visible border in addition to its checked control.
- The homepage includes one Exam Skills entry. The catalogue uses compact rows with Start, Resume or Review and a short description. The topic finder includes canonical activity links and topic aliases. Questions about diffusion distance, heart valves, enzyme inhibition, transcription factors and other concepts link to the relevant Catch-up material. Returning restores the question at the same point.

## Verification and limits

The Chromium checks exercise all authored tasks and drawing variants. They check shuffled choices, checked-state restoration, independent progress, revised written drafts and criteria, marked group rows, saved drawings, contextual return, topic search and actual review outcomes. Browser checks cover Firefox and WebKit through repository workflows, including a restored independent numerical response at a 320-pixel viewport. The full Chromium smoke suite also exercises existing site and app routes.

Responsive checks sample 320, 390, 768 and 1280 pixels. They include keyboard focus, touch, semantic status messages, dark contrast, reduced motion, 200% text sizing and 2× browser viewport zoom. The existing A-level route checks cover Core and Core + Stretch, Paper 1 through Paper 3, practicals, Maths Skills, flashcards, spaced review and settings. Dedicated assistive technology and physical devices were outside this test run.
