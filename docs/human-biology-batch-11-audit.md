# Human Biology Batch 11 teaching audit

## Baseline and method

Baseline is GitHub main at `32debbe` after [Batch 10](human-biology-batch-10.md). Batch 8's existing teaching and Batch 10's separate assessment outcomes are the starting point. The F171 area links a2 and a4 share the teaching and responses for 2.1 and 4.2.2 respectively. There are 18 distinct guided F170/F171 lessons on 20 routes.

Each guided sequence was read in order. The audit checked the objective, prerequisite, scientific explanation, reasoning model and worked example, supported task, independent transfer, error feedback, onward links, navigation and reading demand. The existing common stage navigation is brief, but it gives accordions similar prominence, makes prerequisites easy to miss and initially offers generic links after some errors. The judgements below apply to the stated goal of the lesson. A guided lesson can be effective for its goal while covering only part of the OCR section.

Curriculum reference is [Cambridge OCR Human Biology v8](https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/697578-specification-cambridge-advanced-national-in-human-biology.pdf). The [question writing standard](HUMAN_BIOLOGY_QUESTION_WRITING_STANDARD.md), [question coverage](HUMAN_BIOLOGY_QUESTION_COVERAGE.md) and [quality register](human-biology-curriculum-quality-register.md) describe the linked practice and wider audit limits.

## Guided routes before editing

A = effective for its current objective. B = improve an existing guided sequence. C = recap or important method with insufficient teaching.

| F170 1.1.2 Microscopy | B | A red-cell scale-bar model and conversions support measurement. Resolution and microscope type are mainly explained in prose. The model needs an explicit plausibility check and a route from image size to magnification. | Guided and independent examples both use the same scale-bar operation. Vary the method in independent transfer and use feedback about reversed ratios and mismatched units. Retain magnification and units Skills Practice. |
| F170 1.1.4 Transport | A | Gradient prerequisite, comparison table and oxygen example distinguish particle, membrane and net movement. | The ion question supports gradient reasoning. Water loss and an uncharged solute at equal concentration test transfer with specific feedback and an explanation. |

Add a new delivery scenario and retain cardiac Skills Practice. |
| F170 2.1.3 Gas exchange and delivery | A | Diffusion prerequisite, separate processes and an air-to-tissue example give a causal sequence. | A thicker barrier and then reduced surface area require different reasoning. Keep the alveolar visual and ventilation link. |

| F170 3.2.1 Reflexes | A | Effector prerequisite, synapse explanation, visual and one worked withdrawal sequence clarify direction and function. | Input and synapse failures test upstream/downstream reasoning. Feedback explains what still works. |

| F170 3.3.2 Reproductive hormones | A | Pituitary prerequisite and an FSH–LH–ovulation model separate trigger and result. | Transfer to Leydig cells tests hormone/target reasoning in a different tissue. Preserve the existing F175 model link. |
| F171 2.1 / a2 Treatment choice | B | Stable fracture prerequisite and cast/fixation model connect mechanism and treatment purpose. | Guided reasoning lacks a comparison aid. The written task explains why a drug reaches multiple sites but can be answered without weighing a decision. Use fictional evidence to require a justified and qualified judgement. |
| F171 2.3 Prevention and screening | B | Stages are defined and illustrated, but the embedded follow-up link sits behind an absent prerequisite. | The guided and independent items can be answered by spotting a label. | F171 4.2.2 / a4 Monitoring | B | Repeatability and measurement conditions are well introduced. | F171 a3 Immune response | B | Antigen prerequisite and response model distinguish B cells, T cells and memory broadly. | Matching plasma cells to antibodies requires recall. Model a pathway with distinct actors and ask which downstream event changes when one part is interrupted. |

## Short recaps and audit priorities

The remaining F170 routes are recaps. The priority C routes in this batch are **2.2.2** (cooperation of systems during exercise) and **3.1.2** (interpreting the shape of a glucose-tolerance curve and recognising the limit of that inference). F170 1.1.3, 1.1.5, 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 2.1.1, 2.1.4, 2.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.3.3 and area 4 have selected information or question practice and need wider review in Batch 14. 1.1.1 is a useful prerequisite recap. Wider scope within guided routes, such as the haemocytometer in 1.1.2, also belongs to Batch 14.

The F171 route **a1** is a broad recap. F171 4.1 physiological measurement has a Build Understanding sequence and existing skills but no direct Catch-up teaching route. F171 5.2 clinical research is represented by the a5 lesson; link the exact method to relevant questions. F171 2.2 is a high-priority C gap: explaining how a treatment manages symptoms and evaluating what a change in function demonstrates. Other F171 section-level coverage, including specific diseases, clinical measurements and wider health data, remains Batch 14 work.

Start with the specified pilots and include reproductive tissue identification and screening transfer within their respective F170/F171 phases. Expand into selected C gaps only after the pilot journeys pass.
