## 3. Growth regulator and seedlings: coordinating decisions

- [Opening brief and comparison decisions](09-growth-opening.png)
- [Checked two-concentration comparison and advisory about a zero reference](10-growth-comparison.png)

The exact question is: “Does the concentration of a supplied growth regulator affect the increase in stem length of young seedlings over seven days?” A comparison between the supplied 1 and 5 mg dm⁻³ treatments can address a concentration effect under this wording. Without the 0 mg dm⁻³ treatment it does not compare growth with no added regulator; the activity gives an advisory rather than calling the comparison wrong. The brief supplies individually treated pots and a common dosing and watering schedule.
