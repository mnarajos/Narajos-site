# GCSE Batch 19 implementation and evidence

Batch 18 was merged at 701a43c and had passed repository checks. The audit covered all 21 Trilogy practicals in the revision application, the existing guided catalogue and the linked writing and numerical practice. The [practical register](gcse-batch-19-practicals.md) distinguishes existing teaching, revision questions, new transfer activities and remaining demands.

## Teaching changes

|Subject|Before|After|
|---|---|---|
|Biology|The enzyme route explained effects on activity; the iodine endpoint and interval uncertainty lacked a guided transfer sequence.|Pupils compare 40 s and 80 s endpoints, estimate relative rates of 0.025 and 0.0125 per second, then diagnose a new design where temperature changes with pH.|
|Chemistry|Pupils could predict electrolysis products. This left a gap between prediction and experimental identification.|Pupils distinguish bubbles from a positive oxygen test, then decide why a cathode prediction for molten magnesium chloride changes in aqueous solution.|
|Physics|The forces resources included calculations and revision questions. They offered limited modelling of a change in proportionality.|Pupils subtract an 8 cm unloaded length from a 12 cm loaded length. For a different spring extending 0, 3, 6 and 11 mm, they identify the departure by 3 N and explain why unloading is needed to establish permanent deformation.|

Nine practical sequences address RP4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 18, 19, 20 and 21. Other new teaching covers limiting reactants and plant transport. The latter asks pupils to identify the source of sugar in a leafless bulb. The respiration route traces oxygen delivery through double circulation. An optional Higher glucose-control prompt links glucagon release to negative feedback. Earlier progress keys and question ordering are retained.

The main GCSE bank, its Foundation/Higher settings, spaced revision, practical hub, numerical practice, simulations and Batch 18 lessons are reused. Shared practical reasoning uses the common route. Mole-ratio calculations are labelled Higher; the electrolysis route offers balanced half-equations separately. No confirmed pre-existing incorrect answer key was identified in the reviewed material.

Nine original apparatus or sample diagrams support reasoning. Worked examples have labelled evidence, a guided question and an unfamiliar independent task with a separate retry after help. Feedback distinguishes, for example, a delayed peak reading from random scatter, and limited spatial coverage from a causal claim about moisture. The saved outcome signifies independent selection only when support was not used. Selecting a justified option is less demanding than independently writing the explanation.

## Verification and limitations

Scientific checks included balancing atoms and electric charge, converting mass to moles and centimetres to metres, counting wavelength intervals, comparing endpoint rates, and distinguishing driving weight from resultant force. Specification and question-specific AQA evidence appear in the register. Local static and Chromium tests cover catalogue entry, guided and independent checks, persisted outcomes, revisiting teaching, focus, narrow layouts, reduced-motion settings and representative enlarged-text and axe checks. Repository workflow results for other browsers are recorded on the pull request.

No observed pupil trial took place. Recommended trial: after teaching, ask pupils to design a controlled enzyme comparison, predict the effect of changing a reactant in excess and diagnose a trolley experiment with changing mass. Ask for a reason before offering options. Record anonymous observations separately from the public site.

Next priorities are RP14 energy loss, RP15 wire temperature, RP17 displacement and RP13 purification, followed by comparison of RP11 methods and RP3 controls. Remaining conceptual work includes independent circulation and glucagon explanations and graph transfer involving areas and tangents. Broader application of each practical, including alternative apparatus and extended written evaluation, remains open.
