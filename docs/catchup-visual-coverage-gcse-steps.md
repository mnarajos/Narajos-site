This is a working audit against the exact teaching data, not a claim of completion. “Pending” means not yet checked against the source explanation, the specification, or a rendered page, as appropriate. A mapped figure can support part of a block while leaving a separate fact unrepresented. Opening figures, guided practice and the independent assessment are not assumed to cover teaching steps. Higher Tier entries are recorded separately; their presence does not establish an adequate visual. The text in the second column identifies what students should understand.
