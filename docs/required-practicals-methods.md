# Required practical methods

The apparatus schematics, evidence drawings and plots for these activities are original Narajos materials. The readings are simulated and are labelled as such. The external sources describe the methods. Their figures and photographs were not reproduced.

| Activity | Setup and measurement | Student action | Scientific sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [RP1 · Enzyme activity](https://narajos.com/alevel/#practical-1) | Trypsin digests casein in milk. Identical crosses and viewing conditions define a clearing endpoint after equilibration at each temperature. | Read the first visible cross, calculate reciprocal time and decide how to start a fair temperature comparison. The stretch exercise contrasts endpoint timing with the initial gradient of a continuous trace. | [AQA RP1 apparatus guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P1.PDF), [AQA Biology practical handbook](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-PHBK.PDF) |
| [RP3 · Water potential](https://narajos.com/alevel/#practical-3) | A sucrose dilution series and comparable potato cylinders connect initial and blotted final mass to osmosis. | Calculate a percentage change and interpolate the zero-change concentration. The stretch exercise estimates water potential from the concentration using the ideal dilute solution equation and states the assumptions. | [AQA RP3 apparatus guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P3.PDF), [OpenStax · Transport of water and solutes in plants](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/30-5-transport-of-water-and-solutes-in-plants) |
| [RP4 · Membrane permeability](https://narajos.com/alevel/#practical-4) | Water-bath treatments show pigment leakage from rinsed beetroot pieces across the tonoplast and cell-surface membrane. A distilled-water blank and a 530 nm filter are used for the water-based method. | Select treatments with the same preparation and decide how unequal water volumes limit the comparison. | [AQA RP4 apparatus guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P4.PDF), [Practical Biology · Temperature and plant cell membranes](https://practicalbiology.org/cells-to-systems/cell-structures/investigating-the-effect-of-temperature-on-plant-cell-membranes.html) |
| [RP11 · Glucose calibration](https://narajos.com/alevel/#practical-11) | Quantitative Benedict’s reagent, heat and settling leave a blue supernatant whose absorbance at 680 nm decreases as glucose concentration rises. Distilled water sets the optical reference. The treated zero-glucose standard has its own non-zero reading. | Interpolate an unknown from measured standards and use a second in-range reading to reconstruct the original concentration following dilution. | [AQA RP11 apparatus guide](https://filestore.aqa.org.uk/resources/biology/AQA-7401-7402-P11.PDF), [Mystrica · Benedict’s test](https://www.mystrica.com/Applications/Benedicts), [Hernández-López et al. · Measurement of residual copper sulfate](https://doi.org/10.1021/ACSOMEGA.0C04467) |

The [AQA practical assessment requirements](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/practical-assessment) define the 12 required activities and specify direct observation by teachers for the endorsement.
