# RP2, RP5 and RP6 observation activities

The [AQA practical assessment requirements](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/biology/a-level/biology-7402/specification/practical-assessment) specify a stained plant root-tip squash and mitotic index for RP2, an animal or plant gas exchange or mass transport dissection for RP5, and an aseptic investigation of antimicrobial action on microbial growth for RP6. The sequence, apparatus and terminology were checked against the [AQA apparatus set-up guides](https://www.aqa.org.uk/resources/science/as-and-a-level/biology-7401-7402/teach/practicals-apparatus-set-up-guides). These online activities prepare students to recognise observations and explain the evidence.

The root-tip preparation and inhibition-zone ruler are original SVG schematics in `public/alevel/practical-observations.js`. Both RP2 image crops are practice frames. An estimate for real material would use fields selected in advance from the defined study region, for example with fixed stage increments or random positions, independent of the observed division. The sampling rule uses the position of the nuclear centre or midpoint of the chromosome groups even when the frame cuts the cell wall. It omits unclassifiable cells from both counts and records the omissions.

The RP6 schematic uses six graphic units per millimetre throughout the ruler and zone. The clear-zone edges correspond to 18 and 44 mm, giving a 26 mm total diameter. The reading tolerance is ±1 mm. The worked area includes the disc. The photograph gives evidence of reduced visible growth under the conditions photographed. Differences in diffusion, concentration and assay conditions limit further comparisons.
