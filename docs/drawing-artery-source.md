# Mouse carotid artery · source and calibration

**Observation.** Young unligated control, right carotid artery, transverse section. Elastic van Gieson stain differentiates the dark medial band and the paler tissue outside it. The pupil maps the band and identifies the space it surrounds. The diffuse outer edge of the adventitia, individual cells and debris in the lumen fall outside the stated drawing task.

**Image preparation.** The publisher's PNG is 3,626 × 4,884 pixels. The local WebP retains the first 732 rows at the original resolution, including all four micrographs and the original black bar. The pupil sees a 515 × 515 crop beginning at (310, 145) in the young control panel. The colour and relative geometry of the tissue are preserved. The other three micrographs are outside the displayed field.

**Physical size.** The figure caption explicitly assigns the printed 100 µm bar to all four micrographs. Its dark interior spans x = 3398–3561 inclusive at y = 685, approximately 164 native units. The transitions at either edge are antialiased. Keeping this measurement to whole image units is appropriate to the photographic precision. The 100 µm pupil bar occupies 164 × 300 / 515 ≈ 95.5 drawing units when the field occupies 300. This same ratio applies to photograph, drawing and enlargement.

**Derivation and visual check.** We located the inner and outer edges of the continuous dark band at 36 positions around the lumen, smoothed the outlines and inspected them superimposed on the original photo. At y = 394 the right-hand band lies around x = 702–721. At x = 560 the lower band lies around y = 550–580. The model retains the inward bend on the lower right. The lumen label and its straight line fit inside the open space, with the tip at (505, 394). The proportions variant enlarges the lumen and thins the band. The two-fault variant adds hatching to the lower band and extends the lumen leader too far to the left, ending in the medial tissue at (431, 394).

**Alternative reviewed.** The sham-left-carotid image in Figure 4A of the 2017 Scientific Reports study also has an author-supplied 100 µm bar. A printed measurement marker covers part of the right wall. The 2025 control has an uninterrupted contour of the medial band. [2017 comparison image](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-12321-7)
