Cells and microscopes

How can you recognise a plant cell?

Start here. Find a difference between the outer edges. What else do you see in the leaf cell?

Structures shared by plant and animal cells

The nucleus stores genetic information. The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell. Both structures occur in typical plant and animal cells.

Features to look for in plant cells

A rigid cell wall outside the membrane supports the cell. In green leaf cells, chloroplasts absorb light for photosynthesis. Some plant cells, such as onion bulb cells, usually lack chloroplasts.
