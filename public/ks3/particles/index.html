Particles and gas pressure

Why does gas pressure change?

What causes pressure?

Gas particles move randomly. They collide with the walls and exert a force on them. The force per unit area is the gas pressure.

What happens if we heat the gas?

Heating increases the particles’ average speed. In a sealed, rigid container they hit the walls more often and transfer more momentum per collision. The pressure rises.
