Adaptation and variation

How can an inherited feature become more common?

Start here. Imagine you are a bird looking for food. Which insect would you spot first on each tree?

Individuals vary

Individuals of one species differ. An inherited feature can help an individual survive and reproduce in a particular environment.

Populations change over generations

If individuals with a useful inherited feature leave more offspring, their offspring can inherit it. Over generations, the feature may become more common in the population.

Check your understanding

Light and dark insects of one species live on light bark. Birds spot dark insects more easily. Which insects are more likely to survive?

The bark darkens. How might the population change over generations if colour is inherited?

Try it yourself

In a dry area, some plants have inherited thicker waxy coatings that reduce water loss. Explain why a thicker coating might become more common during many dry seasons.

Compare your explanation

Thicker-coated plants can lose less water and may be more likely to survive and reproduce. Some offspring inherit the coating, so it can become more common over generations.
