KS3 · Explain a change

Why does the food colouring spread faster in the warm water?

See a worked example

Example question: Explain why the pressure rises when air in a sealed rigid container is warmed.

Topic sentence. Warming air in a sealed rigid container increases its pressure.

Notes. Same gas particles and volume → speed increases → more frequent wall collisions → stronger effect per collision.

Closing link. The changed collisions explain the higher pressure.

Warming air in a sealed rigid container increases its pressure. The container keeps the same volume and no air escapes. Heating increases the average speed of the gas particles. They therefore hit the walls more often. Faster particles also transfer more momentum when they collide with a wall. Together these changes increase the pressure on the walls.

Topic sentence to start with: Food colouring spreads faster through warm water.

Help ordering your notes

Start with the warmer water. Explain what happens to particle movement, then link that to how quickly the colour spreads.
