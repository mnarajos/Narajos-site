KS3 · Explain a change

Start here. Point to the beaker where the colour has spread further. Say what you see: “The colour has spread further in the … water.”

Now explain. Why did the colour spread further in the same time?

First time? Look at the worked example before you plan.

See a worked example

Example question
Why does the pressure rise when you warm air in a sealed bottle that cannot expand?

Topic sentence
Warming the air raises the pressure inside the bottle.
Supporting notes
  • The bottle stays the same size and the same particles stay inside.
  • Warmer air particles move faster on average.
  • They hit the walls more often and each hit has a greater effect.
Concluding sentence
The force on each unit of wall area increases, so the pressure rises.
Read the full paragraph

Warming the air raises the pressure inside the bottle. The bottle stays the same size and the same particles stay inside. When the air warms up, the particles move faster on average. They hit the walls more often and each hit has a greater effect. The force on each unit of wall area increases, so the pressure rises.

Plan your paragraph

Write the main idea in one sentence. Jot down what happens first and next. Finish by linking your ideas back to the question.
