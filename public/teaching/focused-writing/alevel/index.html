Worked example question
Explain the importance of the proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Topic sentence
A proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane enables ATP synthesis.
Supporting notes
  • Energy from electron transfer drives proton pumping from the matrix into the intermembrane space.
  • Protons move back into the matrix through ATP synthase, down their electrochemical gradient.
  • ATP synthase uses the energy from proton flow to make ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate.
Concluding sentence
The proton gradient links electron transfer to ATP production for processes such as active transport.
Read the full paragraph

A proton gradient across the inner mitochondrial membrane enables ATP synthesis. Reduced coenzymes donate electrons to the electron transport chain. Energy released during electron transfer drives the pumping of protons from the matrix into the intermembrane space. Protons move back into the matrix through ATP synthase, down their electrochemical gradient. The energy from this flow drives ATP synthesis from ADP and inorganic phosphate. The proton gradient therefore links electron transfer to ATP production for processes such as active transport.

Plan your paragraph

State why the gradient matters. In your notes, trace what the pump does, what moves into the cell and how glucose reaches the blood. End by linking the mechanism to the essay title.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Finish with your concluding sentence. Write it all as one paragraph.
