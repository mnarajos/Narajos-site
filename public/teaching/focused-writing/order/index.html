Use these prompts to check your reasoning against the worked example.

    We do · Sedimentary rock

    How did this sedimentary rock form?

    • Topic sentence Give the main explanation.
    • Supporting sentences Explain what happens to the sediment, step by step.
    • Concluding sentence Bring the steps together and link them to the rock.

    You do · Igneous rock

    How did this igneous rock form?

    Review · Compare the paragraphs

    Both paragraphs open with the main idea, explain the science in order and end by linking the explanation to what you can see.
