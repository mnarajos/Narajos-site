Explain a photosynthesis pattern

Biology Paper 1

Mean oxygen collected from pondweed in one minute
Relative light intensityOxygen (cm³)
Low2
Medium5
High5

The same piece of pondweed was used each time. Temperature and the supply of carbon dioxide were kept the same.

Start here. Where does the amount of oxygen increase? Where does it stay the same?

Now write. Explain the pattern across all three light levels. Suggest one way to test what might be limiting the rate at high light intensity.

Plan your paragraph

Organise your notes in this order. figures → light energy → another possible limiting factor. End with a test.

Compare the low and medium readings first. Then use the high-light reading to identify where the pattern changes.

Help

This is the first sentence of your paragraph. Say the main idea and link it to the question. Try: “The results show that …”

Help

Use a number to describe the increase and another to describe the plateau. Explain why more light changes the first part. Then think about what the plant still needs when light is plentiful.

Help

End with a test. Say what you would change, what you would measure and which light level you would keep.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Write it all as one paragraph. Use an ending if it adds a conclusion or answers a further part of the question.

    Compare with the key ideas
    • Did you use the values? Oxygen collected rose from 2 to 5 cm³ per minute, then stayed at 5 cm³ per minute.
    • Did you explain that more light provides more energy for photosynthesis, so more oxygen is produced per minute?
    • Did you explain that another factor may now limit the rate, for example the supply of carbon dioxide?
    • Did you suggest a test, such as varying the carbon dioxide supply at high light while keeping temperature the same and measuring oxygen per minute again?
    Read a model paragraph

    The rate of photosynthesis increased from low to medium light, then levelled off. In one minute the pondweed produced 2 cm³ of oxygen at low light and 5 cm³ at medium light. More light supplied more energy for photosynthesis. At high light the pondweed still produced 5 cm³. Carbon dioxide supply may now be limiting the rate. To test this, I would increase the carbon dioxide supply at high light and measure the oxygen collected in one minute again.
