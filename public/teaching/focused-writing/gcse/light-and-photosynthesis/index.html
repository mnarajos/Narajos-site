Explain a photosynthesis pattern

Biology Paper 1

Mean oxygen collected from pondweed in one minute
Relative light intensityOxygen (cm³)
Low2
Medium5
High5

The same piece of pondweed was used each time. Temperature and the supply of carbon dioxide were kept the same.

Start here. Where does the amount of oxygen increase? Where does it stay the same?

Now write. Explain the pattern across all three light levels. Suggest one way to test what might be limiting the rate at high light intensity.

Plan your paragraph
Help

This is the first sentence of your paragraph. Say the main idea and link it to the question. Try: “The results show that …”

Help

Use a number to describe the increase and another to describe the plateau. Explain why more light changes the first part. Then think about what the plant still needs when light is plentiful.

Help

End with a test. Say what you would change, what you would measure and which light level you would keep.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Finish with your concluding sentence. Write it all as one paragraph.
