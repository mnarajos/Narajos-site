Plan a fair temperature test

Chemistry Paper 1

Equipment supplied
To measureTo hold or mix
Thermometer, balance, measuring cylinderInsulated cup with a lid, stirrer
Water and two labelled saltsA fresh sample of water for each run

A pupil wants to find out whether two salts give the same temperature change when dissolved in water.

Start here. Which piece of equipment measures temperature? How would you measure equal masses of the salts?

Your job. How would you help a classmate get a fair comparison of the two salts?

Now write. Describe a method to compare the temperature changes. Include what to change, what to keep the same and how to use the readings.

Plan your paragraph

Organise your notes in this order. what to change → what to keep the same → readings before and after mixing. End with repeats and how to compare them.

Help

This is the first sentence of your paragraph. Say the main idea and link it to the question. Try: “The results show that …”

Help

Put the method in time order. Say what you will measure before adding the salt and after. Include the quantities that must stay the same so the comparison is fair.

Help

Explain how to work out the temperature change for each run and how to make the comparison more reliable.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Finish with your concluding sentence. Write it all as one paragraph.

    Compare with the key ideas
    • Did you specify changing only the type of salt while keeping salt mass, water volume, starting temperature and container the same?
    • Did you say to measure the starting temperature, stir in the salt, then record the highest or lowest temperature reached?
    • Did you explain how to calculate the change by subtracting the starting temperature from the highest or lowest reading? A positive change is warming and a negative change is cooling.
    • Did you say to repeat each test with fresh water and compare the mean temperature changes?
    Read a model paragraph

    I would compare the temperature change when each salt dissolves in water. I would measure the same volume of water into the same type of insulated cup and record its starting temperature. I would weigh the same mass of each salt and test them separately. For each run, I would add the salt, stir in the same way, and record the highest or lowest temperature reached. I would subtract the starting temperature from that reading. I would repeat with fresh water at the same starting temperature and compare the mean change for each salt.
