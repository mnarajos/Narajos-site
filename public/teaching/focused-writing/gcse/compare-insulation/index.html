Explain a difference in cooling

Physics Paper 1

Water temperature in two identical lidded metal cans
Covering around the sidesStart (°C)After 10 minutes (°C)
Unwrapped6047

Both cans contained the same volume of water and stood next to each other in the same room.

Start here. Which can had the greater temperature drop? Work out both drops.

Now write. Use the results to explain why felt helped keep the water warm. Suggest how to test whether the number of layers matters.
