Explain a difference in cooling

Physics Paper 1

Water temperature in two identical lidded metal cans
Covering around the sidesStart (°C)After 10 minutes (°C)
Unwrapped6047
One layer of felt6053

Both cans contained the same volume of water and stood next to each other in the same room.

Start here. Which can had the greater temperature drop? Work out both drops.

Your job. Which can would you choose to keep the water warmer after ten minutes? Show how the results support your choice.

Now write. Use the results to explain why felt helped keep the water warm. Suggest how to test whether the number of layers matters.

Write one sentence

      Compare with the key ideas
      • Did you compare a 13 °C drop without felt with a 7 °C drop with felt in the same ten minutes?
      • Did you explain that felt and its trapped air are poor thermal conductors, so energy transfers to the surroundings more slowly through the sides?
      • Did you say to vary the number of felt layers, keep water volume, starting temperature and the can and lid the same, and compare the temperature drop over the same time?
      Read a model paragraph

      The can wrapped in felt cooled less over ten minutes. The water in the unwrapped can cooled by 13 °C, while the water in the wrapped can cooled by 7 °C. Felt traps air and reduces the rate of energy transfer through the sides of the can. I would test different numbers of felt layers on identical cans. I would keep the volume and starting temperature of the water the same and compare the ten-minute temperature drops.
