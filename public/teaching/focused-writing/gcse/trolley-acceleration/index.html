Plan a test of force and acceleration

Physics Paper 2

Assume that friction is small. The pupil can move masses between the trolley and the hanger.

Start here. What pulls the trolley along the track? Where could you move a mass to increase that pull?

Your job. Tell a classmate how to find out if extra pulling force changes acceleration when total moving mass stays the same.

Now write. Describe how to investigate how pulling force affects acceleration while keeping the total mass of the moving system the same.

Plan your paragraph

Organise your notes in this order. vary the pulling force → keep total mass fixed → measure acceleration → repeat and plot your results.

Help

Start with the equipment and the release. Explain how speed measurements give acceleration. Then explain how to change the force while keeping the mass of the whole system fixed.

Say what to repeat and how to use the results to look for a relationship.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Write it all as one paragraph. Use an ending if it adds a conclusion or answers a further part of the question.

    Compare with the key ideas
    • Did you release the trolley from the same point without pushing, and use the motion sensor to find acceleration from the change in speed divided by the time taken?
    • Did you explain that the hanging weight provides the pull, and that transferring masses from trolley to hanger increases it without changing the total mass?
    • Did you say to repeat for several pulling forces, get a mean acceleration for each and compare them, for example by plotting acceleration against force?
    Read a model paragraph

    I would change the pulling force and measure the acceleration of the same total mass. I would move masses from the trolley to the hanger. This raises the weight of the hanger while keeping the total moving mass the same. I would release the trolley from the same point without pushing it and use the motion sensor to record speed against time. The gradient of the speed–time graph gives the acceleration. I would repeat for each force and plot mean acceleration against pulling force.
