Example question
Amylase took 80 seconds at 15 °C and 26 seconds at 35 °C to make the same amount of product. Explain the difference.

Topic sentence
Amylase worked faster at 35 °C than at 15 °C.
Supporting notes
  • The same amount of product formed in less time at 35 °C.
  • Amylase and starch molecules had more kinetic energy at 35 °C.
  • Successful collisions happened more often. More enzyme–substrate complexes formed per second.
Concluding sentence
Amylase worked faster at 35 °C in this test. We would need more temperatures to find its optimum.
Read the full paragraph

Amylase worked faster at 35 °C than at 15 °C. The same amount of product formed in 26 seconds at 35 °C and 80 seconds at 15 °C. At 35 °C, amylase and starch molecules had more kinetic energy. They collided successfully more often, so more enzyme–substrate complexes formed per second. We would need to test more temperatures to find the optimum.

Plan your paragraph

Start with what the results show. In your notes, pair each result with the direction water moved. End with what you would test next.

Help

This is the first sentence of your paragraph. Say the main idea and link it to the question. Try: “The results show that …”

Help

Evidence is what you measured or observed. Reasoning is the science that explains why the evidence supports your answer. Jot down a result with its unit, then add an arrow and the reason it matters.

Help

Finish by answering the question or saying what your evidence lets you conclude. If the question asks what to do next, name the next test.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Finish with your concluding sentence. Write it all as one paragraph.
