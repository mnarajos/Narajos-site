Example question: Amylase makes the same amount of product in 80 seconds at 15 °C and in 26 seconds at 35 °C. Everything else stays the same. At which temperature is the reaction faster? Explain why.

Topic sentence: Amylase worked faster at 35 °C across the two temperatures tested.

Supporting notes: 26 s vs 80 s → same amount of product → faster rate → higher kinetic energy → more frequent successful collisions → more enzyme–substrate complexes per second.

Concluding sentence: The comparison does not establish the optimum.

Amylase worked faster at 35 °C across the two temperatures tested. The same amount of product formed in 26 seconds at 35 °C and in 80 seconds at 15 °C. Producing the same amount in less time gives a greater mean rate. At the higher temperature the enzyme and substrate molecules move faster on average. This increases the frequency of successful collisions and more enzyme–substrate complexes form per second. We would need results from more temperatures to identify the optimum.

Help

Evidence is what you measured or observed. Reasoning is the science that explains why the evidence supports your answer. Jot down a result with its unit, then add an arrow and the reason it matters.

Help

Finish by answering the question or saying what your evidence lets you conclude. If the question asks what to do next, name the next test.

Write your paragraph
Help

Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Finish with your concluding sentence. Write it all as one paragraph.
