      Worked example question
      In a separate osmosis test, equal-sized potato cylinders gained 4% mass in a dilute solution and lost 3% in a concentrated solution. Explain what happened.

      Topic sentence
      The potato cylinders changed mass because water moved into or out of their cells.
      Supporting notes
      • Dilute solution, +4% → net water movement into cells
      • Concentrated solution, −3% → net water movement out of cells
      • Osmosis across partially permeable cell membranes
      Concluding sentence
      Testing intermediate concentrations would help find where there is no net change in mass.
      Read the full paragraph

      The potato cylinders changed mass because water moved into or out of their cells. In dilute solution the cylinders gained 4% mass because more water moved into the cells than out. In concentrated solution they lost 3% because more water moved out than in. The water moved through partially permeable cell membranes by osmosis. Testing solutions between these concentrations would help find where the mass stays the same.

      Plan your paragraph

      For each result, note the solution, the signed percentage and the direction of water movement. End by saying what you would test next.

      Help

      Evidence is what you measured or observed. Reasoning is the science that explains why the evidence supports your answer. Jot down a result with its unit, then add an arrow and the reason it matters.

      Help

      Finish by answering the question or saying what your evidence lets you conclude. If the question asks what to do next, name the next test.

      Write your paragraph

      Help

      Turn your notes into complete sentences. Put your main idea first, then add your supporting sentences. Use links such as “because”, “so” or “however” where they show how ideas fit together. Write it all as one paragraph. Use an ending if it adds a conclusion or answers a further part of the question.
