Compare your reasoning

Did you explain why adding substrate has little effect at saturation and what increasing enzyme concentration changes?

The rate approaches a maximum as the enzyme’s active sites become saturated with substrate. At high substrate concentrations, additional substrate has little effect because almost all sites are occupied. Doubling enzyme concentration provides twice as many active sites, so the maximum rate would double if the other conditions stayed the same.
