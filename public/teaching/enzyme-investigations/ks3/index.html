KS3 · Can the same enzyme break down starch and protein?

Amylase breaks down starch. What do you think it will do to protein?

    You can give your explanation aloud to someone or rehearse it to yourself.

    Check

    • Did you name the substrate amylase breaks down?
    • Did you explain what fits its active site?
    • Can you use the same idea to explain what protease does?

    Model explanation

    Amylase breaks down starch because starch fits its active site. Protein does not fit amylase’s active site, so amylase does not break it down. Protease has a different active site that fits a protein. The two enzymes therefore act on different substrates.
