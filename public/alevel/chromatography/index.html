Results

These chromatograms are simulated: the model's assumptions
  • The method follows the AQA practical handbook: chromatography paper, a pencil starting line 2 cm from the bottom, a leaf disc crushed onto it, 3 cm³ of 1 propanone : 9 petroleum ether, the paper pinned to the bung, and the front marked immediately.
  • For paper and 1:9 propanone:petroleum spirit, Pearson Biology B Core Practical 11 gives reference values: carotene 0.95, phaeophytin 0.83, xanthophyll 0.71, chlorophyll a 0.65 and chlorophyll b 0.45. These are conditional reference values, not universal constants or an AQA table to memorise. Each model run varies slightly; values for propanone alone and water are illustrative.
  • The solvent front rises quickly at first and then more slowly; the paper is 10 cm long above the starting line. Spots spread as they travel, so close pigments overlap on short runs or with large spots.
  • Leaf pigment amounts are illustrative, not measured: the shade-tolerant leaf has relatively more chlorophyll b; the autumn leaf has little chlorophyll left.
  • The close-up is a simplified movement and retention model. Each dot stands for many molecules and its timings are illustrative. The guided route waits for you to mark and outline; any simulated loss represents accelerated evaporation or fading, not a measured deadline.
