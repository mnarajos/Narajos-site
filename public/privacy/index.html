Privacy

A straightforward summary of how these pages and the included apps handle information.

These website pages

The public pages do not require an account or ask you to submit personal details. No advertising trackers or visitor analytics have been added to the website code.

Teaching apps

The GCSE, A-level Biology and Human Biology apps can store settings, revision progress and resumable sessions locally in your browser. Human Biology checks objective answers against predetermined rules; longer written answers are self-assessed against displayed criteria, without AI marking. The apps do not upload revision answers or progress to Narajos servers. Human Biology has a 'Clear progress on this device' control on its homepage; you can also clear locally saved app data using your browser settings. Focused Questions may also save your selected question set in this browser so it can be restored after a refresh. Science Catch-up saves progress and answers in this browser. Earlier saved notes are stored here too.

Hosting and external services

Cloudflare, which hosts this website, may process technical connection information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the service. Google Fonts requests are subject to Google's handling of connection data.

Some pages load the Poppins typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact Google's font servers. The website's privacy information should be reviewed if analytics, accounts, forms, or other data-collection features are added.
