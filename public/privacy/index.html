Privacy

A straightforward summary of how these pages and the included apps handle information.

These website pages

The Narajos portfolio pages do not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information. No visitor analytics or advertising trackers are included in the files supplied in this website package.

Teaching apps

The GCSE and A-level apps can store settings or progress locally in your browser, depending on the app. The supplied app code does not include a feature for uploading those responses to Narajos servers.

Hosting and external services

Web hosting services such as Cloudflare may process technical connection information when you visit the site. Their handling of this information is governed by the relevant service and site configuration.

This is an initial information page for the supplied static website. Review it if you add analytics, account features, embedded media, contact forms or other data collection.
